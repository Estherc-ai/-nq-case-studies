Morning — DEX surge tests the Call Wall, fails to clear it: Following the open-transition reset, price advanced from the 29,474 zone toward 29,574 as 0DTE DEX rebuilt through the 9:45–10:00 AM window. DEX remaining flat at +631.6M into 10:15 AM — rather than continuing to expand — is consistent with the hedge-buying pressure having been absorbed at the wall rather than converting to fuel above it; per the DEX attack/fuel distinction, DEX expansion below a wall functions as pressure against it, while expansion above the level would instead support continuation. Here the level was not cleared, so the accumulated exposure did not transition into the fuel phase.

The subsequent recovery was contained rather than pronounced, with price transitioning into the afternoon range between the 29,374 and 29,474 levels — consistent with exhaustion marking the extent of the downside move rather than producing an immediate directional reversal.
