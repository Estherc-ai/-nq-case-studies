Session Read

Regime. Price traded above Total Gamma Flip and the 0DTE ZG for the entire session, so dealer hedging stayed in positive gamma and the flip was not tested. Price opened 108 points above the 30,709 call wall with no put wall in play. Every print carried positive DEX, and the positive share of the 0DTE book rose from 55% pre-market to 82% at the 10:15 low and 90% at 12:45.

Bottom, 30,709 (approached from above). At 10:15, DEX moved +360.53M → +2.57B, the split 55 / 45 → 82 / 18, SKEW 0.414 → 0.145, and σ 0.35 → 0.21. Under the DEX Attack vs. Fuel rule, DEX expansion with price above a wall is dealer hedging buying (fuel), so SKEW is not required to rise; the rise-with-DEX test applies to a call wall approached from below. VXN reached +2.5σ (21.88) at the low. Price advanced 269 points to the session high.

Top, 30,953 (approached from below). At 11:45 DEX and SKEW both rose into the wall (+1.36B, +0.038; split 75 / 25 → 83 / 17). At the 12:00 touch both fell (−1.59B, −0.029; split 83 / 17 → 70 / 30), the call-side unwind/release direction. From 12:15 to 12:45 the positive share rebuilt to 90% and DEX to +3.96B while SKEW held between 0.066 and 0.142. The high printed at 12:17 and price then ranged inside 30,883–30,933, a 30-point give-back from the high to the close. The magnitude of the 12:00 release is not observable from a single print, and price was absorbed at the largest call wall on both tenors.
