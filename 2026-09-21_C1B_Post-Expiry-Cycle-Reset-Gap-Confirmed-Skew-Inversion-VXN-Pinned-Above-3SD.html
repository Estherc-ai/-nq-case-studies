03 — Session Mechanism: Post-Expiry Squeeze Into Untested Structure, Confirmed Across Three Independent Tools

The session's structural character was set before the New York open. Following the September 18 AM-settled monthly expiration, September 21 opened the first session of a new options cycle — nearby strikes had not yet had the multiple sessions typically required to accumulate the dealer positioning that produces durable resistance. This thin-structure condition is offered as a working hypothesis for the session's behavior across three independently observed signatures — the Gap-Confirmed Skew Inversion pre-market, SKEW and DEX rising together across tenors at the open, and VXN's failure to mean-revert alongside the intraday spread widening near session highs (most squeeze-level strikes held tight spread, consistent with the price advance) — rather than a confirmed rule, pending comparison against the next monthly rollover. The live wall formation (Section 03) is the target this thinness produced, not independent confirming evidence for it.

Price had already cleared the entire 98DTE structural stack during the Asian session, arriving at the New York open (30,222) beyond the 0DTE Call Wall and both 98DTE call OI clusters, placing the session in Condition 1B before the RTH bell rang. The absence of any meaningful pullback at the open — session low (30,219) sitting essentially at the open print — reflected the overnight positioning rather than a fresh RTH-driven move.

VXN — pinned above +3σ, no confirmed exhaustion. VXN swept through the full upside band structure early, breached +3σ (20.12), and reached a session high of 20.72 — a moderate print in absolute volatility terms; the significance lies entirely in the magnitude of deviation from the session's own anchor (19.15), not in an elevated absolute vol level. Through the remainder of the RTH session, VXN made repeated attempts to decline back toward +2σ (19.80) and was bought back up on each attempt — a Repeated Failed Rejection signature, previously documented (Sep 18) only in the decline-then-eventual-break direction. This session showed the variant: VXN never resolved at all, closing at 20.39, still above +3σ, with no ±2σ cross-back confirmed at any point following the sweep. Per the exhaustion hierarchy, that cross-back is the framework's execution trigger for confirmed exhaustion; its absence means the session closed structurally extended and unresolved, not exhausted.

VXN's persistence alongside a rising, non-compressing SKEW throughout a +2.8% cash-index day is consistent with the documented same-direction reading (SKEW elevated, DEX positive, VXN and price both rising = genuine squeeze) extended to its logical extreme: on a move of this magnitude and speed, options-market fear pricing reflects the size of the dislocation itself — protective demand against a snapback from an already-extreme level — rather than opposing the direction of the move.

The session's close (30,782) landed within 20 points of the newest, most credible wall (30,802), pulling back only 82 points (≈0.27%) from the 30,864 high despite the confirmed Call-side Unwind/Release at the close — a materially shallower retracement than the archetype's prior confirmed instance (Sep 16, 2:00 PM), where the same signature coincided with a sharp reversal through multiple structural levels. The divergence is read here as situational rather than a property of the archetype itself: the release coincided with a fresh, sizeable wall (30,802) sitting directly at the point of release, plausibly absorbing what would otherwise have cascaded. The print also arrived close to the session's end, which limits what it can confirm — the true magnitude of dealer unwind is not fully observable from a single closing read, and any remaining exposure may carry into the next session's positioning rather than having resolved within this one.
