03 — Session Mechanism: Range-Bound Between Two VXN-Confirmed Levels, Then a Decay-Driven Break

RTH opened at 29,174 — already below the 0DTE ZG (29,371), a −0.671% distance confirming a negative-gamma open, with price opening just above the 29,171 support level.

Through the morning and into midday, price bounced between two levels: the Total Gamma Flip/Major Wall/ZG resistance cluster (29,321–29,371) above, and the 29,171 support below — unable to clear the top or break the bottom. This range-bound stretch was marked by two VXN spikes to +2SD, one at each end. At 10:46 AM, VXN spiked from near baseline directly to +2SD as price failed its attempt at the resistance cluster. At 12:22 PM, VXN spiked to +2SD again — this time as price touched 29,171 at the bottom of the range. Live volume through this whole stretch read as normal, time-decay-driven — it was the VXN/price timing coincidence, not volume, that marked which touches actually mattered.

Once that second spike fired, the range resolved. Price broke decisively above the resistance cluster in the afternoon — reclaiming Total Gamma Flip, the Major/Call Wall, and the 0DTE ZG together — and ran to a session high of 29,438, closing at 29,431, near the top of the range and just short of the day's Expected Move upside boundary (29,471).

Full Session Chart — MNQU26, 15m: the range-bound stretch between the resistance cluster and 29,171 support, followed by the afternoon break above ZG, Major/Call Wall, and Total Gamma Flip into the close.
This session provides the cleanest evidence yet for a core principle: VXN's SD levels signal exhaustion — how stretched the current move is — not direction. The identical +2SD level fired twice in one day, once at the top of the range (a resistance rejection) and once at the bottom (a support rejection). If +2SD carried any inherent directional meaning, it could not have marked both a ceiling and a floor on the same session. What it actually confirmed both times was magnitude of tension in the moment — whether that tension resolves as a rejection at a high or a low depends entirely on where price is positioned relative to the framework's structural levels (walls, ZG, Total Gamma Flip) at the time the spike fires, never on the VXN reading alone. Every VXN extreme should be read jointly with the price-structure context it coincides with — never as a standalone directional signal.
