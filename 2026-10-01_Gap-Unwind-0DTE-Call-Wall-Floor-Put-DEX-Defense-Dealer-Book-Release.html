🟨 9:45 — Overnight gap unwound; options book still long calls

The Micron move to ≈ 31,149 was in futures. The fade to ≈ 30,846 before the open was futures traders taking profit; NDX options do not trade overnight, so the options book did not change during the run. At 9:45 the 0DTE book was still call-heavy (+936.7M, 67 / 33), and SKEW had moved from −0.043 to 0.286: call holders kept the upside and bought protection into ISM instead of closing. The overnight fade was the first warning; calls held with protection bought set up the unwind.

🟨 10:15 — Lower call wall: put DEX heavy across most strikes

ISM at 10:00 unwound the remaining call delta. The 30,612 strike — the largest 0DTE call DEX at 9:45 (≈ +450M) — fell to ≈ +110M, and price reached it before the 10:15 print. At 10:15, DEX had flipped to −2.71B with put DEX heavy across most strikes around and below price (30,672 ≈ −250M, 30,572 ≈ −222M, 30,722 ≈ −175M, 30,622 ≈ −146M). With about three-quarters of the call delta at 30,612 already unwound (+450M → +110M), the call-side hedge selling was largely spent, and the defense was layered under the low. VXN rose to the +2.5σ zone on the drop. 30,612 held as the floor through three tests; on the 11:11 sweep to 30,531, VXN tagged +3σ (≈ 23.8), and the sweep was reclaimed as VXN crossed back below +3σ at 11:14. From 10:30 to 11:30, DEX cycled between defense and exhaustion — absorption. Through the range, the call DEX at 30,612 rebuilt from ≈ +110M (10:15) to +173M (11:30), +295M (12:45) and +598M (1:15): call positioning at the floor while price chopped, with the range allowing size to be built without moving price.

🟨 1:15 — Release: negative billions flipped, SKEW near zero

The floor held for about three hours (10:00–10:15 to 1:15). DEX stayed negative in the billions through the absorption, reaching −7.83B at 1:00. At 1:15 it flipped to +2.42B: the put DEX above price that had capped every rebound disappeared (≈ −220M to −270M per strike → ≈ −100M), SKEW fell to 0.011 (no put premium), and σ turned up from 0.20 to 0.24. Put holders closed, dealers bought back their short-futures hedges, and call buying turned the call DEX above price into fuel. Price recovered the session to 30,883, rejected at the 30,872 take-profit zone with VXN at the anchor, and settled back toward the ZG (close 30,770).
