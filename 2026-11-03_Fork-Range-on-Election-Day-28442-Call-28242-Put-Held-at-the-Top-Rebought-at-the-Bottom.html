1 — Market Context

US midterm election day; results were due after the close. Oil had dropped more than 7% on Monday on Iran deal hopes, then swung overnight before settling about 1% lower. Apple fell after its earnings (a disappointing holiday-quarter margin outlook), while chip stocks rebounded and led the Nasdaq; AMD reported after the close. The Nasdaq Composite finished +0.83%. NQ had closed Monday's cash session at 28,224, swept the 0DTE Put Wall overnight (≈ 28,030 at about 2:30 ET), and traded ≈ 28,347 pre-market.

The downside had already been reached overnight, in the NQ futures session before the cash open — the open print had to show where the upside destination was, and how far.

The day in brief. Pre-market was a Fork — a book with its largest call above price and its largest put below, so price is pulled toward the bigger, growing side first, and swings between the two when they are similar in size: here the call 28,692 above and the put 28,042–28,052 below, similar in size: a balanced, two-way book that fit a session waiting for the midterm results — and the overnight session had already swept the downside. The open print added a new largest call, 28,442, only 50 above price, while the far wall at 28,692 shrank: a short leg, not a trend. Below, the put at 28,242 (0DTE + 7DTE + 98DTE) held the open low, 28,212, and grew into the opposite magnet. 🟨 10:45 — 28,442 grew for a third print while 28,242 stopped growing: the Fork leg confirmed, and price tested the 28,342 floor at 10:46 and ran to 28,442. 🟨 11:00 — the high, 28,445, with protection still held: no cash, and the release of the 28,392 floor at 11:30 turned it. 🟨 12:00 — protection cashed at 28,242, then was rebought on the next print: the range held. Price swung between the two magnets, 28,242–28,442, for the rest of the session, with the bodies closing under the 7DTE lid at 28,418, and closed at 28,312 into the election results.

Blue arrow — 28,392: the lid switched to a floor. Pre-market, 28,392 was a put above price (0DTE −69M · 7DTE ≈ −115M) — a lid; the 8:30 spike was rejected there. From the 10:00 print it turned into calls (+42M → +120M → +310M at 11:00), and once price traded above it, those calls were the floor under the run to 28,442. Its release at 11:30 (−55%) ended the up leg.

Why it switched, in two parts: 1. Mechanical: as price comes up to a put strike, the put's delta shrinks while the call at the same strike gains delta, so the net DEX tilts toward calls. 2. Real flow: the calls grew far bigger than the original put (+310M vs −69M), so traders built calls at that level. Dealers hedge those calls by buying as price dips toward it, which is the support.
