1 — Market Context

Tuesday followed the record Monday rally. Before the open, Nasdaq-100 futures gave back about 2–2.5%, led by the chip names that had led the day before (Nvidia, Micron, Intel); HPE fell about 11% premarket after its results. PCE, GDP and durable goods were scheduled for 8:30 ET on Wednesday, ahead of the Thursday holiday.

NQ opened at 26,363 — 437 under Monday's close (26,800) — after an overnight low of 26,147. The high, 26,513, came at 10:00; the low, 25,998, at 11:15. Close 26,230: −570 (−2.13%).

Three things decided the day: the book was put-side from the first print and never went above zero; the box that formed at 9:45 was a thin band of calls inside that put book; and the largest strike moved below price at 10:00 and stayed the destination all session.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Put Lid · Hedged (9:30) → Inverted Range (9:45) → Put Chase · Unhedged (10:30) → Unwind (12:00).

2 — Day Focus: a False Door, a Destination That Never Moved, and a Real Unwind

  1. The book set the side from the first print. −2.34B at 9:31, never above zero after. Every door had to be read against it.
  2. The box was weak by construction. At 9:45 the calls holding the floor were about a third of the lid's size, with puts on both sides of them.
  3. The largest strike told the direction before price did. At 10:00 — the print of the high — 26,070 doubled below. From then it was the destination on every print.
  4. The unwind came only when the move was finished. Bought at the destination, then cashed to near zero, with nothing growing beyond.
