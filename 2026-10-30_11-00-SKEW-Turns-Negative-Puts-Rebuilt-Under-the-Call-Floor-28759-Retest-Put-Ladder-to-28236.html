⬛ The Day in Three Parts: the 9:45 Destination, the 11:00 Negative SKEW Turn, the Put Ladder into 28,236

9:45 — the open print named the destination below. DEX deepened from −2.72B (pre-market) to −5.48B with SKEW at 0.024 — directional puts, no insurance. The pair below price set the path: 28,759 (−205M) the block at price, 28,659 (−255M) the destination. The session high, 28,859, came at 9:45 and again at 10:00; the low of the first leg was 28,667 at 10:00, 8 above the destination.

10:00 → 10:45 — the bounce inside the Box. Calls were built at the low (28,739–28,859, ≈ +530M) and, at 10:45, the book flipped call-side: DEX +2.76B, the 28,959 put turned into a +125M call, and the put stack below price released by about half. But the largest strike on the book stayed the 29,159 put above price (−335M) and SKEW stayed at 0.028 — no protection at the floor. A call floor below price and the largest put above it formed a Box: the flip was the dealer rebalancing at the top of the bounce, not new call buying.

🟨 11:00 — SKEW turned negative for the first time. SKEW 0.028 → −0.019, from a session in which protection had never been held. With calls priced over puts and little demand for put protection, the put DEX on the book was more likely directional — and it was rebuilt directly under the new call floor: 28,759 −255M → −328M, 28,659 −165M → −220M. The calls above shrank (28,859 +145M → +60M) and DEX added only +0.35B after the 10:45 flip. Price broke through 28,759 inside the 11:00–11:15 interval, closing below it.

🟨 11:15 — the retest of 28,759. The broken strike became the largest on the book (−600M), above price. Price returned to 28,759 from below and was rejected; the whole book was put-side (DEX −5.83B), the call floor released by half and the 28,959 call was gone.

11:15 → 14:45 — the put ladder. Each destination below price grew while price was above it, was swept, and became the next lid: 28,659 (swept 11:30–11:45, rejected from below at 12:00) → 28,559 (13:00) → 28,459 (13:15–14:00) → 28,359 (14:15). SKEW stayed negative on every print except 13:45 (+0.021, one print), and σ rose from 0.31 to 0.42 into the afternoon. The low, 28,236 at 14:45, came at the 28,259 put (−700M at 14:15; NDX 28,100).

14:00 → 4:00 — the lids released from the top down. With price far below them, the largest put lids shrank: 28,759 (≈ −1.71B → ≈ 0) at 14:00, 28,659 (≈ −2.05B → a small call) at 14:15 — put holders closing. Price recovered from the 14:45 low and closed at 28,556, 3 under the 28,559 strike.
