The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Fork (9:45) → Put Chase (10:30) · Unhedged.

What a Fork is. A Fork is a day when the largest 0DTE call DEX sits above price and the largest 0DTE put DEX sits below it, at a similar size (within about 2×). Both strikes act as magnets: each pulls price toward it, so price swings between the two with no direction, and the book can flip from print to print. A Fork resolves only when one magnet is released — it drops to half of its own peak or less while the other holds or grows. The remaining magnet becomes the destination, and the day turns into a Chase toward it. SKEW confirms how the move goes: with protection held (0.10 or more) the move is slow, with pauses; with SKEW thin or about 0 nothing cushions the market, and the break is fast.

Fork vs Inverted Range. The two look alike, but the strikes sit the other way round. In an Inverted Range the put is above and the call below, so the big strikes are edges that hold price in a box. In a Fork the call is above and the put below, so the big strikes are magnets that pull price out toward one of them. Cashed means protection (puts) was sold; protection bought means puts were bought as insurance.
