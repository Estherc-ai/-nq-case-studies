03 — Session Mechanism: A Double-Leg Decline With ZG Swept, Not Just Rejected

RTH opened at 28,540, already below ZG (28,625) — a mild Condition 3 open. The first 15-minute candle pulled price up toward ZG, reaching 28,621 — just below it, no touch — before price turned lower. As the decline built, a stacked live OI concentration formed at 28,363 / 28,330 / 28,323 — three levels bunched tightly together, not present in that form pre-market — and price pushed through it to a first low of 28,254 around 10:45 AM.

From there, price reversed and this time swept through ZG outright, printing the session high of 28,631 around 12:00 PM — 6 points above ZG, not merely a failed approach like the first attempt. Worth being precise about what happened mechanically: ZG itself doesn't "flip" mid-session — it's a fixed structural level for the day, marking where dealer gamma exposure transitions from net short (below) to net long (above). Sweeping through it briefly doesn't mean the regime changed; price didn't hold above it long enough to establish itself in the positive-gamma side, and it rejected shortly after — well short of the 0DTE Call Wall (28,680), which was never seriously tested.

What the Compression Actually Reflects Mechanically

Elevated post-open skew (0.161) meant dealers were still pricing meaningful downside protection demand, consistent with a session that had opened in negative-gamma territory. That put-heavy positioning meant dealers were short gamma on the downside, which amplifies moves (their hedging adds to selling pressure on any decline). As skew compressed toward 0.095, that put-side pressure was unwinding — the market was pricing less residual fear, which is part of what allowed the reversal off the first low to extend all the way through ZG rather than stalling below it. This is the same mechanism from the Jul 22 case study (skew compression capping a range), running in the opposite direction here — compression enabling the move rather than capping it.

After the 12:00 PM ZG rejection, a new live put wall formed at 28,161 as dealers repositioned following the sweep. Price then declined a second time, breaking back down through the same territory, and bottomed at a session low of 28,212 around 3:15 PM — stopping just short of the new 28,161 wall and never reaching the deeper pre-market convergence zone (Asian 1SD lower 28,112.36 / IV 1-Day EM lower 28,127) below it. From there, price stalled rather than reversing again: it held near the low for the remainder of the session, closing at 28,307, only marginally above the low print.

The close landing back inside the 28,363–28,323 stack zone (formed during the first leg down) is notable in its own right — that zone effectively bracketed price for the rest of the session even after being broken through twice. A level that gets breached on the way down and still ends up as the session's closing gravitational pull is a stronger read on genuine depth than a level tested once and simply holding.

The vol side's relationship to price was asymmetric across the two legs. Around the 12:00 PM high, VXN dipped toward its −2SD level (27.44) — a lagging signal, arriving after price had already topped and swept ZG, not before. Then, as the second decline built toward its conclusion, VXN spiked back up toward +2SD (29.24) ahead of price finishing its move — a leading signal into the 3:15 PM low.

Reclassification — Dual Bookend With Asymmetric Timing

By session end, VXN had touched both +2SD (29.24) and −2SD (27.44), fully bracketing NQ's own round trip from 28,212 to 28,631 — the same dual-bookend pattern documented in the Jul 21 case study. What's new here is that the two touches weren't symmetric in timing: VXN lagged at the high and led at the low. That means VXN's predictive value wasn't constant through the session — it only confirmed after the fact at the top, but front-ran the move at the bottom. Worth tracking whether this lead/lag asymmetry repeats on future sessions.

03a — Context: No Friday Max Pain Gravity

Friday Max Pain (28,840) was never a realistic target — price traded in a roughly 28,212–28,631 range the entire session, several hundred points below it, and 0DTE time decay worked against any reversion attempt as the day progressed. This matches the same Friday Max Pain failure pattern documented in the Jul 10 and Jul 17 case studies: on 0DTE expiry days, Put Wall / Call Wall / ZG structure governs the session far more reliably than Max Pain gravity, which only tends to matter when price starts the day genuinely close to it.
