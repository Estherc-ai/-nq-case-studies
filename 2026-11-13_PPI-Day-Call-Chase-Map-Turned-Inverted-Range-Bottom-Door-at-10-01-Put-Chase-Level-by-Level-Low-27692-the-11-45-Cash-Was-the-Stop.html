1 — Market Context

October PPI was released at 8:30 ET, before the open; a December rate hike remained close to fully priced after the hot CPI earlier in the week. Premarket attention was also on an Nvidia leadership change and a Microsoft service outage. NQ traded near 28,543 before the data and opened at 28,391, 42 under Thursday's close (28,433). The session closed at 27,950, −483 (−1.70%).

The pre-market map read as a Call Chase; the first print after the data read as an Inverted Range — and the open print led.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Inverted Range (9:45) → Put Chase (10:01) · Hedged → cashed at 11:45 → back to the box bottom. An Inverted Range is a day when the largest 0DTE put DEX sits above price and the largest 0DTE call DEX sits below it; price is held in a box between the nearest put above and the nearest put below until one side releases. A Put Chase is a day when price is pulled to the largest put below it, level by level. Hedged means SKEW stayed at 0.10 or above, so protection was held; cashed means that protection was sold.

  • Pre-market: a Call Chase shape. The largest 0DTE call was above price at 28,693 (+197M), also the GEX Call Wall; the largest 0DTE put was below at 28,303 (−47M). The call was about 4× the put, so one side was clearly bigger: a Chase. The 98DTE wall at 28,603 (TGF + −1.2B) sat between price and the destination — Pattern B, "held first, then ladder." SKEW was 0.051 (no protection).
  • 🟨 9:45: the first print confirmed an Inverted Range. The 28,693 call released −78% (+212M → +47M). The largest put was now above price at 28,303 (−111M) — the box top — and the largest call below at 27,983–27,993 (+156M). The box bottom was the nearest put below, 28,053 (−44M). Protection was bought into the open (SKEW 0.051 → 0.147).
  • 🟨 10:01: the bottom door opened. The floor call released (27,983–27,993 +156M → +87M, then flipped to puts), the puts below grew, and the box bottom broke: retest at 10:02, one small pullback to 28,033, then the Put Chase level by level — 28,003 → 27,903 → 27,853 → 27,803. Each broken level was retested from below as the lid.
  • 10:00 → 11:45: a hedged Put Chase. SKEW stayed at 0.13–0.16, so protection was held the whole way down; 27,853 — a put (−82M → −128M) — was the ceiling throughout, until it flipped to a call at 11:30. The destination was the largest put below, 27,703 (−388M at 11:15); the session low, 27,692, swept it by 11 in a brief move that did not hold.
  • 🟨 11:45: cashed at the bottom. SKEW fell from 0.148 to 0.091, and to 0.052 at 12:00 — the protection held all morning was sold. The cash marked the stop: the puts below released by half, calls built under price, and 27,853 — already flipped to a call — broke from below and became the floor.
  • Afternoon: back to the box bottom. Price returned to 28,053 and swept it a few times (≈ 28,110 late) but never closed back inside the box. Close 27,950, under the box bottom.
