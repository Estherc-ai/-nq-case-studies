04 — Session Mechanism: Pin, Grind, and a Clean Uptrend Confirmed by VXN Collapse

Price dipped early to a session low of 28,313.75, landing almost exactly on the Asian −1SD band (28,297.10) — a real statistical floor holding on first test.

From that low, the session turned into a sustained, one-directional grind higher — through the 1SD baseline (28,567), through both Call Walls (28,523.02 and 28,673.02). Along the way, two new live levels formed intraday: a huge live volume concentration at 28,718 and a live Call Wall at 28,818 — both above the pre-market Call Wall cluster, consistent with your "price chases volume" pattern from earlier in the week. Price pulled back to test a Put Wall before reversing and chasing that live call structure higher, continuing to a session high of 28,964.50 — just above the live 28,818 Call Wall and the 2-Day Expected Move (28,923.02), short of the 3-Day level (29,023.02). The session then closed back near the 2-Day Expected Move, holding the bulk of the day's gains rather than fully round-tripping.

VXN's behavior this session was the mirror image of the squeeze mechanics documented earlier this week (Jul 30). Rather than rising with price, VXN collapsed steadily throughout the session — briefly testing toward +1SD/+2SD during New York AM, then declining through baseline, −1SD, −2SD, and ultimately sweeping through −3SD (25.17) into New York PM, with wicks trading even below that level before a modest recovery into the close. This is the textbook inverse relationship: a clean, un-contested uptrend with genuinely falling implied volatility, not a call-driven squeeze forcing dealer hedging. Worth contrasting directly with Jul 30 — same broad outcome (price grinding higher all session) produced by two structurally different mechanisms, distinguishable only by watching whether VXN rises or falls alongside the move.
