03 — Session Mechanism: Open Sweep of the Call Wall Cluster, Live Put-Volume Absorption Holds the Floor, VXN's 20-Minute Struggle Confirms the Low

RTH opened at 29,439 — already −0.502% below the 0DTE ZG (29,587), a negative-gamma open. Within the first three minutes, price pushed up to 29,517, wicking through the confirmed live call wall (29,504) and into the stacked cluster zone (29,504–29,557) without holding — a brief sweep-and-reject that confirmed the cluster's resistance. Price then reversed hard from that opening sweep, falling to a session low of 29,354 by approximately 9:33 AM.

That low is notable for what it did not reach: the Major/Call Wall (29,332) and Total Gamma Flip (29,327) — both sitting only 22–27 points further below — were never tested. What held price above them was a genuine live put-side volume concentration at 29,354–29,355, confirmed building in real time as price approached. This wasn't a static pre-market level defending the floor — it was live absorption: price testing the level repeatedly over an extended stretch, with the selling pressure absorbed each time before finally exhausting itself. The zone held through every retest without giving ground.

VXN's behavior through this stretch is equally significant — and harder to read in real time. From the session low onward, VXN spent approximately 20 minutes trading above +2SD (21.17), struggling to push through to the +2.5σ level (21.26). At the exact moment NQ printed its session low (29,354), VXN reached 21.25 — one tick below +2.5σ — before reversing. This is the documented "+2.5σ struggle" pattern: the level does not need to be breached to act as an exhaustion ceiling. The sustained above-+2SD grind, unable to break higher, was itself the exhaustion signal — the vol-side confirmation that the put-volume absorption below was genuine, not just a temporary pause. Once both signals fired together (vol absorption exhausted, VXN stalling just below +2.5σ), price began its slow recovery.

The afternoon session saw a steady grind back up through the zone, recovering into the call wall cluster (29,504–29,557) by the close. The session ended at 29,522 — inside the cluster zone, above the live call wall (29,504), and well above every pre-market floor level.
