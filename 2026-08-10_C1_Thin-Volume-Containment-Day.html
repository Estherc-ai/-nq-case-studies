03 — Session Mechanism: A Range Day Defended by Many Small Walls, Not One Large One

RTH opened at 29,806 — essentially on top of ZG (29,765), a +0.138% near-ZG open carrying no directional bias into the session. Price spent the day oscillating between the 0DTE Call Wall (29,865) above and ZG (29,765) below, briefly overshooting both boundaries — a high of 29,900 (beyond the Call Wall) and a low of 29,720 (beyond ZG) — without ever escaping the broader range. The session closed back almost exactly at ZG, the same level it had opened near.

The defining mechanical feature of this session, distinct from every prior case study this cycle: containment came from a stack of small, distributed put and call walls rather than one dominant level. A live-observed GEX volume lock-in zone formed between roughly 29,713 and 29,869 — not a single pre-labeled wall, but a cluster of smaller strikes each holding a modest amount of OI. Overall volume through the session was thin, with two notable volume spikes early in the day and comparatively little participation afterward.

This is a direct, real-time confirmation of the wall-vs-volume race rule from the opposite direction of how it's usually observed. Prior sessions (Aug 6, Aug 7) showed a large wall surviving because GEX growth outpaced strong incoming volume. Today showed the mirror case: even small, individually unremarkable walls were sufficient to contain price, because volume attacking them was thin all session. It doesn't take a large wall to hold when there's barely any force attacking it — the race was won by default on both sides, not by size.

New session archetype worth naming distinctly from the three prior case studies this cycle: Aug 5 was a sweep-and-reject at a dominant single wall; Aug 6 was a live-forming wall producing a sharp reversal; Aug 7 was a repeated-test-then-breakout at a confluence level. Today is a thin-volume containment day — no single wall did the defending, and no breakout occurred, because the session simply lacked the volume to test any level hard enough to matter. The pre-session calm skew read (0DTE compressing further by 9:45 AM) was consistent with this outcome from the start.
