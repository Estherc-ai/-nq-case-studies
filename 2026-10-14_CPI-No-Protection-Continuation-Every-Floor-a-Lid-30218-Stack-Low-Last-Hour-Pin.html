  • Last-hour mechanics: as 0DTE expiry nears, gamma concentrates at the strikes nearest price and far strikes decay toward zero. The call wall and put wall move to the nearest strikes and the range narrows around price — the pin. At 16:00 the 0DTE options expire and the pin ends.
  • In the money = hedge complete. Delta ≈ −1: the futures are already sold, so a further drop adds no selling at that strike.
  • Large DEX ≠ new pressure. The 14:00 print (−45.68B) was mostly finished hedges above price (30,138–30,143 −5.4B, 30,218 −4.6B).

  • Oct 13: the puts already owned were closed at the open; the hedges against them were bought back and price ran ≈ 190.
  • Oct 14: nothing to close. New, cheap puts opened below price — a ladder of strikes under price.

The dealer side: a dealer short a put is long delta. As price falls toward the strike the delta grows and the hedge is to sell futures; below zero gamma that hedging runs with the move. Each new put strike below price became the next target: 30,522 → 30,472 → 30,422 → 30,372 → 30,318 → 30,218 → 30,128.
