⬛ The Day in Three Parts: the Map, the FOMC Pin, the Release

The map — two destinations. The open print confirmed the upside one: 30,084 grew from +148M to +454M at 9:33, the largest strike on the board. The downside one, 29,679, became the biggest floor below price and grew all session (+70M → +165M).

The FOMC pin — the book hedged instead of chasing. From 9:45 to 2:30 price held a range of 29,849–29,919. Protection was bought and rebought (SKEW 0.12–0.19) and σ rose print after print into the decision (0.36 → 0.53). The largest strike moved to the pin itself (29,859 / 29,909), and price stayed with it. Two reversal warnings — 13:30 and 14:15, the floor flipped to puts with puts built below — were both undone on the next print.

The release. After the press conference the protection was cashed in one print (SKEW 0.190 → 0.056) with the biggest DEX jump of the day (+5.88B) — the climax print at 14:45. At 15:00 the calls above shrank and protection was rebought; at 15:15 the destination zone released (30,069 −62%). The 0DTE book expired at the 16:00 cash close: 30,084 was swept, high 30,181 at 16:00, then in the extended session price fell to the largest floor below — low 29,674 at 16:45, 5 under 29,679. The futures session closed at 29,755, on the TGF band.

VXN marked the high. On the blend engine VXN fell through −2SD into −3SD at 16:00 — the volatility low at the 30,181 high — then turned back above −2SD as price fell to 29,674.

Both pre-market destinations were reached — at the end of the session, not in the morning. On a decision day the map held; the timing moved to the expiry.

Both levels were reached between the 16:00 cash close and the 17:00 futures close, so the session chart includes the extended hours.
