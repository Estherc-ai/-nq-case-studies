At 2:06 PM — six minutes after the FOMC release — VXN swept −1σ (21.21), briefly dipping below the already-low morning floor at the exact moment a major scheduled catalyst had just been absorbed. Read in isolation, a −1σ touch is unremarkable; read in context, a fresh low in fear arriving in the same minute window as a market-moving announcement is a genuine complacency snap — the options market briefly had not yet caught up to the incoming information. VXN then reversed sharply, pushing through +1σ, +2σ, and toward +3σ (~23.2) during the afternoon as the real reaction took hold, before easing back to close at 22.44.

One caveat worth noting: VXN is a mid-quote-derived index and can be partially influenced by bid-ask spread mechanics around a news release, unlike DEX and SKEW which require real capital and cannot be spoofed. The −1σ dip should be read as a minor, contextual confirmation of the complacency-into-the-event pattern rather than a pure, capital-backed sentiment signal on its own — the subsequent violent move to +3σ is the stronger, more reliable confirmation that genuine information was being absorbed.

Price opened at 29,386, essentially pinned to the 0DTE Gamma Flip. By 10:00 AM, 0DTE DEX had fully sign-flipped from a negative pre-market baseline to +659M, with SKEW rising in tandem (0.059 → 0.163) — the same-direction DEX/SKEW movement the framework's Call Wall matrix identifies as a genuine squeeze rather than a mechanical surge. This same-direction read applies specifically to the 0DTE tenor; the aggregate DEX across all tracked tenors actually fell over this same window (from roughly +5.56B pre-market to +2.59B at 10:00 AM), almost entirely because the Sep 18 AM monthly position collapsed from +6.97B to +2.73B as it re-marked against the newly-opened live price — a separate, mechanical rebalancing event rather than a signal of weakening conviction. The 0DTE-specific squeeze read, confirmed by its own DEX/SKEW pairing, is the one that matters for interpreting the price action that followed. DEX continued building to +1.04B by 10:15 AM with SKEW climbing further to its session peak (0.176). This genuine, DEX-and-SKEW-confirmed squeeze phase carried price up through ZG and through the moderate, dual-confirmed 98DTE/0DTE call concentration stack (29,407.72 / 29,437.72), cleared without significant resistance.

At 10:30 AM, DEX and SKEW eased together (+784M, 0.159) — a brief, matched pause rather than a reversal. The subsequent push to the session high was mechanically distinct from the earlier squeeze: by 11:45 AM, 0DTE DEX had rebuilt sharply to +1.47B, while SKEW dropped (0.158) rather than rising alongside it. Per the framework's Call Wall matrix, DEX rising while SKEW drops is the Call Hedge Exhaustion signature — a mechanical push lacking fresh conviction behind it, rather than a continuation of genuine squeeze pressure. The Sep 18 AM monthly contract moved the same way over this exact window, nearly doubling from +4.6B to +8.74B — the massive structural position was building in lockstep with the 0DTE exhaustion, reinforcing that this final leg was a broad mechanical push across the whole positioning structure rather than a thin, 0DTE-only phenomenon. This distinction explains the session's structure well: price reached the Expected Move upside boundary (29,537.72) for a session high of 29,552, but stalled there rather than continuing toward the far larger 98DTE Major Call Wall (29,582.72) — consistent with the final leg being mechanical rather than conviction-driven. Front-end σ did rise into this high (0.35 → 0.42), but this is consistent with event-anticipation vol building into the approaching 2:00 PM catalyst rather than confirmation of the price move itself. VXN's own behavior over this same stretch reinforces the read directly: it stayed confined between the anchor and −1σ for the entire morning, never showing fear even as the mechanical push carried price to its high (Section 02). The remainder of the morning ranged between the 29,437 call concentration and the 29,537 Expected Move boundary — a contained pre-event box, with no further conviction-driven push in either direction.

Full Session Chart — MNQZ26, 15m

At 2:00 PM, coinciding with the FOMC decision release, 0DTE DEX pulled back sharply from its session-high peak (+1.47B → +903M) while SKEW dropped further (0.158 → 0.127) — DEX now actively falling alongside falling SKEW, rather than merely failing to confirm as it had at 11:45 AM. This is a distinct quadrant from the earlier Call Hedge Exhaustion signature: where the 11:45 AM print showed the exhaustion building (DEX still rising, unconfirmed by SKEW), the 2:00 PM print showed it releasing (DEX and SKEW both actively declining together) — the Call Wall matrix's Call-side Unwind/Release signature, the genuine mechanical unwind of the position that had carried price to the high. Front-end σ simultaneously spiked to 0.61, the sharpest single reading of the day, consistent with the market repricing real volatility as the decision's information was absorbed. The Sep 18 AM monthly flow eased in parallel (+8.74B → +7.28B), confirming the unwind was broad-based across the whole positioning structure rather than confined to 0DTE alone. VXN added a further, independent layer at this exact juncture: at 2:06 PM it briefly swept below even its already-low morning floor to −1σ (21.21) — a momentary complacency snap — before reversing violently toward +3σ as the real reaction took hold (Section 02).

This 2:00 PM print stands as strong independent evidence for what followed. The DEX/SKEW data did not merely accompany the reversal — it told the story of the reversal before the full price move had played out: a call-side position that had built without fresh conviction (11:45 AM) was now visibly unwinding at the exact moment the market received the information it had been waiting for (2:00 PM), fully consistent with the framework's own documented pattern that a genuine Unwind/Release at a call wall precedes a fast reversal. Price then confirmed this reading in full: it touched the Expected Move boundary one final time before reversing sharply, falling through the Asian Open SD baseline (29,268.75), the 98DTE/0DTE Put Wall (29,307.72), and continuing to sweep through the 0DTE Put concentration at 29,107.72 — a level identified in the pre-market map as an immediate structural reference — before printing the session low at 29,053. Price then recovered back toward and just below the Asian Open SD baseline, closing at 29,264.

This session is a clean example of a pre-event squeeze-and-reversal archetype, distinct from the framework's existing Gamma Squeeze case (Sep 3), where the squeeze continued through the dominant wall rather than stalling at a statistical boundary ahead of a scheduled catalyst. The mechanical drivers of the morning advance — dealer delta-hedging as price rose, reinforced by momentum-following flow — were genuine and confirmed by DEX/SKEW, but structurally distinct from the directional conviction that ultimately resolved the session at the actual announcement. The pre-event advance functioned as a liquidity window rather than a resolution of the day's true direction, with the real move only arriving once the event itself removed the uncertainty.

06Pre-Market vs Full Session Comparison

Every level identified pre-market played a genuine role in the session, on both sides of price. The session closed almost exactly at the Asian Open SD baseline, the same independently-calculated level that has now shown genuine gravitational relevance across multiple consecutive sessions this cycle.
