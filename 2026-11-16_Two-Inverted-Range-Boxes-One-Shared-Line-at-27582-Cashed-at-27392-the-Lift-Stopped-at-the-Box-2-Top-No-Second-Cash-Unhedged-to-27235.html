The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Inverted Range (9:45) → Put Chase (≈10:00) · Hedged → Inverted Range Box 2 (11:00) → Put Chase (12:15) · Unhedged. An Inverted Range is a day when the largest 0DTE put DEX sits above price and the largest 0DTE call DEX sits below it; price is held in a box between the nearest put above and the nearest put below until one side releases — the door. A Put Chase is a day when price is pulled to the largest put below it, level by level. Hedged means SKEW held at 0.10 or above (protection in place); Unhedged means it did not. Cashed means that protection was sold — dealers buy back their futures hedges, and that buying is the fuel for a lift.

  • Pre-market: a Call Chase shape. The largest 0DTE call was above price at 28,292 (+200M), also the GEX Call Wall. Between price and that destination sat the TGF and the 98DTE Put Wall at 28,092 (≈ −3.0B). SKEW was 0.509 — heavy protection.
  • Overnight gap down. NQ was at 27,592 before the open, 358 under Friday's close and below the whole lower map, with every put above price.
  • 🟨 9:45: the open print confirmed an Inverted Range. The 28,292 call released −93%; puts stacked above price and a call floor sat below. Box 1: 27,582 ↔ 27,732. The open high, 27,717, stopped 15 under the box top.
  • 🟨 ≈ 10:00: the bottom door. Price swept the Expected Move bottom, 27,492, and came back; the 10:00 print then showed the floor call released −83% (27,612–642 +93M → +16M) and new puts below. The Put Chase ran to 27,392, the last put before the new call floor at 27,342–352.
  • 🟨 10:15: cashed at the destination. SKEW fell from 0.184 to 0.083 — the protection held since the open was sold. The cash marked the stop and fuelled the lift back to ≈ 27,609.
  • 🟨 11:00: the box moved down. Box 2: 27,447 ↔ 27,582 — 27,582, the Box 1 bottom, became the Box 2 top. Price was rejected there three times; an 11:30 break above it returned inside the box within minutes.
  • The cash lifted, but couldn't reverse it. The lift stopped at 27,582; SKEW stayed thin (0.05–0.08) for 2½ hours, so there was no second cash and no second lift — and no new call demand above.
  • 🟨 12:15: Box 2 broke down (confirmed on the 12:30 print). The Put Chase returned to 27,392; protection was bought back there at 13:15 (SKEW 0.084 → 0.098) — a brake, not a lift.
  • 🟨 ≈ 15:30: SKEW turned negative (−0.010) and the session low printed, 27,235 — the unhedged last leg swept the 27,242 put by 7. Close 27,299, on the 27,292 put.
