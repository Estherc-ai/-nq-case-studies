The largest 0DTE and 98DTE walls sat at different strikes. Method used each session:

  1. Compare the 0DTE, 1DTE and 98DTE GEX walls and find the largest wall nearest the open, above and below.
  2. Check whether that wall is also a DEX-heavy strike.
  3. Watch the first test after the open, then confirm with the next DEX/SKEW print.

4 — DEX/SKEW at the Three Turning Points

DEX/SKEW changes support a rejection at all three levels. The evidence is strongest at the 10:45 low.

1. Open, sweep of 30,708: supported

  • Pre-market was +1.68B, call-weighted 73 / 27, with SKEW at 0.088, below the 0.10 floor. That is a large call book with no skew behind it, which is the Call Hedge Exhaustion risk profile.
  • By 9:45 DEX had flipped to −2.16B while SKEW stayed flat (0.088 → 0.090). No conviction built up at the wall, so the call-side positioning had nothing to push price through 30,708.
  • Limitation: there's no 9:30 print, so the rejection itself isn't observed. It is inferred from the prints before and after. The pre-market figure is also OI while 9:45 is OI + volume, so the size of the flip is only approximate. The direction is clear.

2. 10:45 low, sweep of 30,377: strongly supported

The prints show the full matrix sequence around the low:

  • 10:00 to 10:45 was a mechanical cascade. DEX went from −1.31B to −12.32B while SKEW fell from 0.091 to 0.042. SKEW never rose on the way down, so no fresh put conviction was behind the move. It was hedging flow, which exhausts once it reaches concentrated strikes.
  • At 10:45 the 0DTE put mass sat on the walls: 30,483 at −755M and 30,383 / 30,377 at −595M. The low swept 30,377 by 20 and stopped there.
  • 11:00 was a genuine defense. DEX peaked at −16.2B and SKEW rose to 0.058, the only meaningful SKEW rise of the morning. Price made no new low.
  • 11:15 was exhaustion. DEX fell by 7.28B and SKEW fell to 0.029. The 30,483 bar dropped from −767M to −214M as price reclaimed it.
  • Why 30,483 broke first but 30,377 held: 30,483 was broken during the mechanical cascade. The defense and the exhaustion then printed after the 30,377 sweep.
  • Limitation: the confirmation comes 15–30 minutes after the low, so it lags the turn.

3. 12:15, second sweep of 30,708

  • The matrix cell is DEX more negative with SKEW falling, which is technically mechanical. The DEX change was only −50M, though, so this isn't a cascade.
  • SKEW fell to 0.017, the lowest reading of the session and close to zero. Puts and calls were priced almost equally, so there was no fear premium in either direction.
  • With no conviction on the call side or the put side, nothing was pushing price toward either wall. That matches the range from 12:15 to the close.
  • The front end kept losing call weight. The 2DTE went from +922M to +670M (60 / 40), and 1DTE stayed flat at 51 / 49.

After 12:15 price rejected 30,708 and then ranged; it didn't reverse. The prints show the same thing:

  • A reversal would need conviction. At a call wall, that means DEX dropping sharply with SKEW confirming, or a new put build. At 12:30, DEX moved only −50M and SKEW fell to 0.017, so no side was taking control.
  • The walls weren't the same strength. At the open, 30,708 met a positive, call-weighted book, and the rejection turned into a 403-point decline. At 12:15 the book was already put-dominant (35 / 65) and unwinding. The wall capped the move, but nothing was left to push price lower.
  • The close fits a range. Price closed at 30,558, inside the 30,483–30,708 box, well above 30,483 and without testing 30,377 again.

Magnitude caveat: SKEW stayed below 0.10 all morning, and most of the SKEW changes are only 0.001–0.010. The only readings large enough to count are 10:15 (−0.041), 11:00 (+0.016) and 11:15 (−0.029), and those three are the ones the read above depends on.
