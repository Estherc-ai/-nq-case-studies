1 — Market Context

Veterans Day: the stock market was open, the bond market closed, and no data was scheduled. Overnight, Nasdaq-100 futures rebounded about 1.9% on technology: CoreWeave rose about 13% on a profitability outlook that eased concerns about AI spending, Nvidia rebounded about 5% after two down sessions, and chipmakers led. A December rate hike remained close to fully priced after Tuesday's CPI report. In the NQ futures session price opened at 28,352, 494 above Tuesday's close, and closed at 28,462 (+604, +2.17%).

By the open, price sat above every level on the pre-market map — the Call Wall, the 98DTE Put Wall and the expected-move top.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Call Chase (9:30) → Inverted Range (9:45) → Call Chase (10:27) · Hedged — a gap up above the whole map, a Call Chase for one print, an Inverted Range for 42 minutes, then call DEX added above price rung by rung — one cash at the 11:00 climax with thick calls beyond (a pause), then held until 13:45. Hedged means SKEW stayed at 0.10 or above, so the protection was still held. A Call Chase is a day when price is pulled to the largest call above it, rung by rung; an Inverted Range is a put lid above price and a call floor below, holding price between them.

  • 🟨 9:30 — gap up, Call Chase for 15 minutes. Price opened at 28,352, 494 above Tuesday's close and above every pre-market level. The open print turned call-side above the gap: floor 28,272 (+475M), a call 43 above at 28,397 (+120M). READY LONG.
  • 🟨 9:45 → 10:27 — Inverted Range for 42 minutes (three prints). 28,397 released by half (+120 → +35M) and a put lid opened at 28,347 (−115M), with a second stack at price (28,277–28,297, together ≈ −290M). Price held between the 28,272 floor and the lid; the low, 28,200, came at 9:45.
  • 🟨 10:27 — the lid break. The lid shrank on every print (−115 → −85 → −58M) and disappeared on the live book as price broke through it; at 10:30 the puts at price flipped to calls and 28,397 grew to +280M — the range became a Call Chase.
  • 🟨 10:30 → 13:45 — call DEX added rung by rung. Each destination became the next floor: 28,397 → 28,497 → 28,597 → 28,647 → 28,697.
  • 🟨 11:00 — climax + cash, but a pause. The largest jump so far (+7.12B) with SKEW cashed (0.096). On a climax + cash print the calls above decide: thin = the top; thick and growing = a pause. The calls beyond were thick (28,567–28,597 ≈ +1.9B) — some holders took profit, but the demand was still there. The pullback held 28,497 and protection was bought back on the next print.
  • 🟨 12:45 — climax held. The largest jump of the day (+8.06B) under 28,647; SKEW 0.103 → 0.139 — no cash from 11:15 to 13:30.
  • 🟨 13:45 — the cash after the held stretch (SKEW 0.046). One minute later, the high, 28,697; then a reversal to the close, 28,462, on the 28,447–28,497 floors.

Why the cash marked the high. The cash is the moment the large holders sell their protection — the position is ending. Before it, they kept the long and the insurance; the dealers kept their hedge, the call DEX stayed on the book, and every pullback was bought at the floors. At the cash the insurance was sold, the dealers unwound, and the support under price went — nothing was left to push, so the high printed and the reversal followed. However late it came, the session waited for it.
