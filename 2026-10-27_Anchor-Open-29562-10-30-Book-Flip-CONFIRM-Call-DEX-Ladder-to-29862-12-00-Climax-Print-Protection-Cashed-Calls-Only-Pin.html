⬛ The Day in Three Prints: 9:33 Anchor, 10:30 CONFIRM, 12:00 Climax Print

9:33 — no direction yet. NQ opened at 29,560, on the largest 0DTE Call DEX (29,562). The open print grew it and stacked calls under price, but built nothing larger above — an anchor with no destination. Price ranged around it and pulled back to 29,494 at 10:15.

10:30 — CONFIRM, the book flipped. DEX swung +3.0B in one print: the 29,462 put flipped to a call, the put stack below released, and a call ladder formed above price — 29,612 → 29,662 → 29,762 → 29,812. Price stepped up the ladder rung by rung.

12:00 — destination confirmed at 29,862: the climax print. The biggest DEX jump of the day (+5.58B) printed as 29,862 was tagged, with protection cashed (SKEW 0.096) and nothing larger beyond.

  • A climax print is a warning, not a signal on its own. It marks exhaustion of the push; the stop is confirmed only when it coincides with the destination tag and the protection cash. At 11:30 a climax-size jump (+3.56B) came with 29,812 untagged and protection still held — the move continued.
  • The stop ends the advance; it does not imply a reversal. A reversal requires the book itself to turn: put DEX built below price and held for two or more prints. Protection rebought and calls released after the stop are supporting signs, not the deciding one.
  • Oct 27 did not meet that condition — the book stayed calls-only. Protection was rebought and the top calls were released (12:15–12:45), yet no put DEX was built or held on any print. With call DEX on both sides of price and nothing on the put side, the move after the stop was a pullback to the call floors and a pin around the largest strike, not a reversal. The afternoon followed that book: price held the 29,762 floor, rotated around 29,812 with no 15-minute close above 29,862, swept the 29,882 rung once at 15:45, and closed at 29,798 — 14 points under the largest strike.
