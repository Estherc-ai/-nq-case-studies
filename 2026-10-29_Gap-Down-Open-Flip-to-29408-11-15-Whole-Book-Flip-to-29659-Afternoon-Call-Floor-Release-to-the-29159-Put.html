⬛ The Day in Three Parts: 9:30 Open Flip, 11:15 Book Flip, the Floor Release into the Expiry

9:30 — the open print flipped the book, because of the overnight gap. The pre-market DEX was the open interest from the Oct 28 close — positions built with price near 30,000–30,180, its largest call (30,149) already swept at that close. The ≈ 580 drop came overnight, when only futures trade: dealers re-hedged with futures, but the options book could not change until the cash open. At 9:30 the first flow traded at the new, lower price — puts bought below it with no put premium, directional rather than hedging — and the book turned put-side in one print. DEX swung from +772M (pre-market) to −1.90B and SKEW collapsed to 0.010 — no protection, directional puts. The largest strikes were puts below price: 29,534 (−365M) and 29,434 (−230M), the nearer one the block, the farther one the destination. Price swept through both to 29,408 at ≈ 9:40.

9:45 — the floor was built at the low. Both put destinations were cut in half and calls appeared at the sweep low — 29,419 / 29,429 (+410 / +460M), the largest strikes on the book — with protection bought (SKEW 0.010 → 0.124, σ flat). Price never returned to them in the morning: pullbacks held at 29,442–29,449 and 29,489, above the stack.

11:15 — the whole book flipped, with the destination close. DEX swung +4.25B to +2.10B, protection was cashed (SKEW 0.109 → 0.085), the put stack at 29,434–29,459 released by half or more and the puts at price turned to calls. The destination, 29,659 (+325M), was 60 points away and nothing larger sat beyond it. The large call stack below (≈ +1.03B) did not release — it stayed the largest weight on the book, three times the destination, so the move up was short. 29,659 was swept within the next bar, high 29,677; the 12:00 retest came with protection cashed at the destination (SKEW 0.069) and 12:15 released it (−70%).

Why a call-side flip still ended in a down day. - A flip confirms the move to its destination, not the day's direction. The 11:15 flip pointed to 29,659; it was reached, and the 12:00 cash at the destination ended it. - The move ran against the gap. Price stayed below both gamma flips (29,749 / 29,799) and below the pre-market calls all session — negative gamma, inside a day that opened ≈ 580 under the prior high. The rise to 29,677 was a pullback up, not a trend change. - After the stop, the book decided. 12:15 released the destination (−70%) and the calls above; from 13:15 put DEX was built below price and held on the next prints (29,459 to −997M) — the book itself turned. Price then waited on the call stack until it released into the expiry.

Two signals, two meanings.

The cash at 11:15 fuelled the move; the call stack below held and kept supporting price, but its size against the destination kept the move short. The same stack limited the downside all afternoon — the drop came only when it released into the expiry.

14:00 → 4:00 — the floor held, then released into the expiry. Through the afternoon put DEX was added below price on every print — 29,459 grew to −997M, the largest strike on the book — and from 14:00 the call stack at 29,419–29,429 shrank by about half. The 14:00 print confirmed the book on the put side, but with little room: the destination, 29,459, sat only 17 points below price. But the stack still held price: from 13:45 to 15:45 price could not get below it. The release came only into the 4:00 expiry; with the floor gone, nothing held price until the round-number put at 29,159 (NDX 29,000), the largest put DEX below price in the end-of-day book. Low 29,113 at 4:00, close 29,182.
