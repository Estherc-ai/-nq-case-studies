The session opened at 28,906.50, roughly 1.6% below Friday's close, with price declining further through the first minutes of trading before finding a floor near the session low (28,877.75), in the vicinity of the lower 180DTE immediate open-interest clusters — the only structure present anywhere near the open, given the entire 98DTE/0DTE map sat far overhead. The SD baseline (~29,070), sitting well above this floor, was not tested until the subsequent recovery phase.

Phase 1 (pre-market → open): 0DTE DEX flipped sharply from a mild positive pre-market baseline (+306M, composition 56% call / 44% put) to a deeply put-dominant −2.36B by the first 10:00 AM print (composition 8% call / 92% put) — a single sharp flip coinciding with the pre-market decline, rather than a gradual intraday build.

Phase 2 (10:00–11:00 AM): 0DTE DEX held in a stable, deeply negative band (−1.79B to −2.21B) for a full hour, with composition consistently put-dominant (85–92% put) and SKEW rising over the same window (0.231 → 0.314) — a signature of active, held defense rather than mechanical decay, consistent with the framework's quality-filter rule that sustained DEX alongside rising SKEW indicates genuine conviction. Price traded in a range near the lower immediate OI clusters during this phase, testing down toward the 180DTE OI cluster near 28,771 without a clean breakdown, and had not yet reclaimed the SD baseline.

Phase 3 (11:15–12:00 PM): DEX began genuinely shrinking (−1.91B → −1.70B) while SKEW simultaneously collapsed (0.314 → 0.129), and by approximately noon both metrics had fully normalized — DEX near flat (−48M to −454M range), composition rebalanced to a near-even 42% call / 58% put, and SKEW flattened to 0.060. This three-way confirmation (composition, magnitude, and surface fear all normalizing together) marked the exhaustion of the earlier put-side defense. Price began advancing through the immediate OI clusters and toward the overhead 98DTE structural stack coincident with this exhaustion signature.
