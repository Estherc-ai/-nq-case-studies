1 — Market Context

NQ closed Thursday at 26,594 and kept falling overnight: by 8:10 it was at 26,086, 508 under the prior close and under Thursday's low. Two things decided the day: the OPEX unwind lifted price into the block and stopped there, and the Put Chase that followed went down one rung at a time with no protection bought at any destination.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Put Lid · Hedged (9:30) → OPEX unwind (9:45) → Put Chase · Unhedged (10:12).

A Put Lid is a day when price sits below all the big 0DTE put DEX: the puts act as lids above it. A Put Chase is a day when the largest put sits below price and pulls it down. Hedged means protection (puts bought as insurance, SKEW ≥ 0.10) is held; Unhedged means the move runs with none behind it. Cashed means that protection was sold; released means a strike has fallen to half of its own peak or less. The unwind is the covering lift when hedges come off. The block is the put stack that stops it. A rung is one 0DTE put strike on the way down. ZG is the 0DTE gamma flip; TGF is the total gamma flip.

2 — Day Focus: the OPEX Unwind, Then a Put Chase Rung by Rung

  1. Open: Put Lid · Hedged. Price was under all the big puts with protection held. Puts above = lids, nothing big below.
  2. 9:45: the OPEX unwind. The AM monthly settled at the open, the PM protection was cashed, and the lid puts at 26,283 and 26,583 were released. The dealers' hedges against those puts came off, which lifted price.
  3. 10:00: the top at the block. The lift stopped at the 26,433–26,483 stack, the block, with the cash already used. The 26,583 Put Wall was never reached.
  4. 10:12: the break. Price fell back through 26,283, the lower edge of the lid zone, and retested it from below at 10:13: Put Chase · Unhedged.
  5. 10:15 → 12:30: rung by rung. Each print named the next put below as the destination. Price reached it, SKEW showed nothing bought, and the next rung grew.

  • The cash was the fuel. Protection held pre-market (0.254) was sold at the open (0.053 by 9:45). Puts sold means the dealers buy back their short futures hedge: that is the lift.
  • The stack added up bigger than any single put. 26,433 + 26,458 + 26,483 ≈ −358M together, more than the 26,583 wall alone. The top came at the stack, not at the wall.
  • The book disagreed with price at 10:00. Price rose +279 while the book went 1.9B more negative, and the 26,583 put → call switch reversed on the next print (−124M at 10:15). The unwind was spent.
