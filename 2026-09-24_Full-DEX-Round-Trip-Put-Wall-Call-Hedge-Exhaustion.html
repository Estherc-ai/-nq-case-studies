Close: confirmed reversal, not unresolved. The fade from the session high back to 30,735 is the predicted outcome of the Call Hedge Exhaustion signature (per the standing rule: expect a fast reversal, do not chase the move) rather than an ambiguous ending — plausibly reinforced by ordinary 0DTE time decay weakening the wall's defensive strength through the afternoon. Standing Methodological Notes From This Session

Tenor rotation. Which tenor (98DTE, 1DTE, or 0DTE) carries the largest, most prominent wall at any given moment is not fixed — it rotates intraday as price moves and each tenor's own book is recalculated. The Cross-Expiry Wall Confirmation check should be re-run at each new decision point rather than assumed to still be dominated by whichever tenor held the largest wall earlier in the session.

SKEW floor effect. SKEW rarely sustains below ~0.10 — a print this low (0.060 at 10:00 AM) reverted into the 0.1x range on the very next read (0.139), consistent with an inherent floor (little room left to compress once puts and calls are pricing almost identically) rather than requiring a location-specific explanation.

Unwind magnitude remains unobservable from a single print. A confirmed genuine release (DEX and SKEW both falling) establishes the nature of a move but never its remaining size — the standing dealer book isn't directly visible, so each confirmed signature should be read as "real, but of unknown remaining magnitude."
