🟨 10:00 — Destination confirmed

The open was already through the 0DTE walls, so the first job was to find where the 0DTE flow was heading. On the live chart the largest call wall at 9:32 was 31,597. On GBR, the 0DTE call DEX at 31,350 NDX (31,599 NQ) grew from ≈ +370M (9:45) to ≈ +600M (10:00) while price moved only ≈ 35 points — new calls, not just delta. Live wall and DEX growth at the same strike = the destination. The larger 98DTE bar at 31,325 NDX (31,574 NQ) was longer-dated (Nov): it paused price on the way up but did not stop it.

🟨 11:15 — Exhaustion at the sweep

SKEW followed the sequence at the wall: 0.0xx → spike to 0.105 at 10:30 (protection bought, not ready) → back down to 0.057 at 11:00 as price reached 31,600. At 11:15 price swept to 31,616 with DEX at +17.83B, σ at the session low (0.14) and SKEW 0.078 (+0.021, noise-level, below the spike): ready. The 15-minute candle at 11:00 had closed above 31,600 — a trap; after the 11:15 print price closed back below 31,600 on the 1-minute chart and did not reclaim it. The 11:30 print (first DEX drop) confirmed the peak.

🟨 11:45 — Call-book unwind

DEX fell from +17.83B to +631M by 12:30 and later turned negative with put DEX building, while SKEW rose from 0.078 to 0.190 — put premium returning as the call book unwound. σ stayed flat (0.14–0.15): an orderly unwind. Rebounds stalled at the slow 31,574 level; the close was 31,501.
