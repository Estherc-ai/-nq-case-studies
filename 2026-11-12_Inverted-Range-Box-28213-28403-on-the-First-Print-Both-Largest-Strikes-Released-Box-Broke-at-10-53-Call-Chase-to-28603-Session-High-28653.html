The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Inverted Range (9:45) → Call Chase (10:53) · Hedged. An Inverted Range is a day when the largest 0DTE put DEX sits above price and the largest 0DTE call DEX sits below it. Price is held in a box between the nearest put above and the nearest put below until one side releases. A Call Chase is a day when price is pulled to the largest call above it, rung by rung. Hedged means SKEW stayed at 0.10 or above for most of the session, so protection was held.

  • 🟨 9:45: first print, an Inverted Range. The largest 0DTE put DEX was above price at 28,513 (−320M); the largest call DEX was below at 27,853 (+405M). The box ran from the nearest put above, 28,403 (−80M), to the nearest put below, 28,213 (−65M).
  • 9:45 → 10:53: inside the box. The high, ≈ 28,390, stopped under 28,403, and the low, ≈ 28,202, swept 28,213. The calls inside (28,303, 28,373) acted as pins or were passed through.
  • 🟨 10:45: both of the largest strikes released. The largest put above, 28,513, went −320M → −65M, and the largest call below, 27,853, went +405M → 0. The lid layer at 28,473–28,483 released by half (−225 → −110M), and 28,373 became the largest call above price. Only the box top, 28,403 (−155M), was left.
  • 🟨 10:53: the box broke. 28,403 gave way, and price went through 28,513 (ZG + TGF + 0DTE Put Wall) into the Call Chase: 28,543 → 28,603.
  • 🟨 11:00: climax print (+997M → +9.91B, SKEW cashed at 0.046). The 0DTE put at 28,603 flipped to a call (+485M), which became the destination. Destination reached with the climax and the cash, and the 28,603 put flipped to a call — the confirmed top. The session high, 28,653, came at 11:15, 50 through it. The Call Chase ran until ≈ 11:20.
  • Afternoon: a pullback and pin at 28,513–28,533. Close 28,433, 30 above the box top.
