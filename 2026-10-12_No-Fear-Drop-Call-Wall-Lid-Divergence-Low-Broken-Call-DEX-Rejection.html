The two largest strikes: DEX vs GEX

NQ (NDX) 0DTE DEX(OI) 0DTE GEX Type

30,959 (30,730) +80M call (largest bar) +40M (half the wall) DEX only: in the money at the 8:00 pricing, so delta inflated the bar
  • DEX = delta × open interest: the hedge size held at a strike, i.e. where the money sits.
  • GEX = gamma × open interest: how fast that hedge changes as price moves. Gamma peaks at the money, so the largest GEX strike is the wall where price pins or rejects.
  • An in-the-money strike carries a large DEX (delta near 1) but a small GEX. A large DEX bar with small GEX is a weak level.

🟨 9:30 — Open below the call wall

  • 31,129 was the 0DTE call wall (largest call GEX, with call DEX and a 98DTE level on the same strike).
  • Friday closed on it, and the overnight session sat at it before slipping 60 points below by 8:58. The open (31,045) came in below it, under the flips and the 31,089 / 31,099 in-the-money puts.
  • The book leaned to calls, but those calls sat above price. A call wall above price is a lid.
  • The open was the session high (31,050 at 9:30). Price stepped down through the 31,004–31,029 cluster (lost by 9:45) to the EM bottom.
  • 30,959, the largest DEX bar, was DEX-only with half the GEX of the wall: a weak floor. It broke at 10:00–10:15, and the morning low came near 30,858.

Why both became resistance: - 30,959 (lower edge): a call DEX strike that broke from above at 10:00–10:15. With only half the GEX of the wall it was a weak floor. Once price was below it, call delta collapsed and the dealer long hedge was sold, so the retest could not reclaim it. - 30,979 (upper edge): a put-heavy 98DTE level sitting above price after the drop, a lid that carries across sessions. The live 0DTE call there grew to +250M at 12:15: call DEX above price is a destination, and calls were monetized at it.

Call DEX vs put DEX by location:

Below price Above price
Call DEX Floor while held. Once broken, call delta collapses and the dealer long hedge is sold, so it is hard to reclaim and becomes a lid. Destination; calls are monetized there, so it acts as resistance.
Put DEX Destination and support; puts are monetized and the dealer short hedge is bought back. In the money = lid.

The 12:15 print was still positive (+4.29B). The 12:30 print confirmed the turn: - DEX: +4.29B → −278M. - 30,929: +850M → +72M. - 30,949 / 30,959 / 30,979 flipped from calls to puts. - SKEW −0.025 → 0.040 (call premium gone); σ 0.12 → 0.13.

By 12:45 an in-the-money put stack (30,929–30,989) capped price. The afternoon pinned inside 30,879–30,899, the session low printed at 30,843 (14:45) and the close was 30,877, on the live put-heavy 30,879.
