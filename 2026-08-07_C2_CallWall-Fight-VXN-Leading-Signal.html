The first test rejected, and price reversed hard back down through the Call Cluster (29,631) and through ZG (29,601) itself, briefly undercutting it to a session low of 29,564 — a genuine, if shallow, dip below the gamma-flip level.

This is where VXN's early behavior became the key tell. Instead, VXN broke down almost immediately and continued sliding through −1SD (23.03), −2SD (22.67), toward −3SD (22.32), and never reverted back up for the rest of the session — even as price was still undercutting ZG and testing the low at 29,564. Each retest built on the last, and price eventually cleared 29,821 decisively, running to a session high of 29,861 and closing at 29,840 — above the confluence level, not just wicking through it. The strength continued into the following session (Monday, Aug 10), extending toward the longer-dated 98DTE Call Wall (30,072).
