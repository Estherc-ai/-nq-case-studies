1 — Market Context

The October CPI report, released at 8:30 ET, came in above forecast: headline +0.4% month over month against 0.3% expected, core +0.3% against 0.2%. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.00% and December rate-hike expectations firmed. Technology was the weakest sector (−1.13%); Nvidia fell 5.5%. The Nasdaq Composite closed −0.74%, the S&P 500 −0.38% and the Dow −0.30%. In the NQ futures session the release dropped price about 300 points, from the 27,9xx area to the 27,602 98DTE put level, 30 above the expected-move bottom.

By the open the pre-market destination above had been released, and the book had two edges: a put lid above price and a call far below.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Inverted Range · Hedged — an Inverted Range is a book with a call floor below price and a put lid above, both holding price in; Hedged means protection stayed on the book through the morning (SKEW at or above 0.10, near the line in the afternoon), so every leg turned and none became a trend. The lid was the largest put above price, 28,102 — on TGF and the −3.1B 98DTE Put Wall. The floor was a new call far below, 27,102 (+320M). Price stayed between them all session.

  • 🟨 9:30 — the open print set the type: the pre-market Call Wall 28,242 released −85%, puts sat above price from 27,852 to 28,202 (largest 28,102, −165M), the puts below were thin (27,602 −25M), a new call 27,102 (+320M) opened far below, and SKEW rose to 0.203 — protection bought on the news and held. An Inverted Range · Hedged.
  • 🟨 9:45 — protection was cashed (0.203 → 0.054), the book flipped call-side (+2.75B), the puts below disappeared and a new call opened above, 28,022 (+278M): the hedge put on for the CPI drop came off — the unwind V.
  • 🟨 10:00 — 28,022 reached (+584M, the largest strike) with the 28,102 put lid 80 above; protection was bought back (0.054 → 0.194). The high, 28,092, stopped 10 under the lid.
  • 🟨 10:30 — the calls above released by half (28,242 −51%, 28,302 −75%) and a put opened at ZG, 27,902 (−190M), which grew to the session's largest strike (−1.8B): the lid of every move after it.
  • 🟨 10:45 → 12:45 — selling absorbed above the CPI low; the deepest put DEX of the day (−15.5B) came with σ at its peak (0.33) and the low, 27,518 (12:30), 16 above the 27,502 put. Both breaks of 27,602 failed.
  • 🟨 13:45 — the puts below released by half (27,502 −54%, 27,602 −46%), the put mass sat above price, nothing below. Price drifted back to close at 27,858, under ZG 27,912. The 27,102 floor call was never reached.

How the Inverted Range formed at the open print. Before the release the map pointed up, to the 28,242 Call Wall. The open print showed what remained after it: the Call Wall released −85%, the puts bought into the drop sat above price as lids — the largest on the 28,102 block — and a large call, 27,102, had opened far below, where the pre-market book held about +5M. The puts below price were thin. A call floor below, a put lid above and protection held: an Inverted Range · Hedged, with its two edges set at 9:30.

The edges are limits, not targets. In an Inverted Range price is held inside the edges, and whether an edge is touched depends on how close and how heavy it is. The lid, 28,102, was near and heavy — price came within 10 of it. The floor, 27,102, was far below and past the expected move — a backstop, not a destination; each drop turned earlier, at the heavy puts inside the range (27,602, 27,502) and the expected-move bottom. Inside the range every leg turned at a heavy strike: 28,022 at the top, 27,902 on the pullback, 27,502 at the bottom.
