1 — Market Context

The second session after the US midterm elections, in which AP projections showed Democrats winning both chambers. AI and chip stocks led a broad sell-off on fears of tighter oversight of AI and data-center build-out: the Nasdaq-100 index (NDX) closed −6.36% at 26,629.81 (−1,807.36 points), its worst single day since 2020; the Nasdaq Composite closed −5.52%, the S&P 500 −4.36% and the Dow −3.44% (−1,833 points). QQQ fell 6.49%, and VIX rose about 69% to 28.18. In the NQ futures session, NQ had closed Wednesday's cash session at 28,627 and traded ≈ 28,345 before the bell.

The pre-market book was put-heavy with negative SKEW — no protection bought. In the NQ futures session, one call floor, 28,318, was the only support under price (≈ 28,345 before the bell).

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Put Chase · Unhedged — a Put Chase is a book whose largest strike is a put below price, with nothing of similar size pulling from above — here the open print showed the largest call above, 28,938, released by ≈ 70% — so price is pulled down to it; Unhedged means SKEW stayed below 0.10 with no protection bought, so nothing slowed the move and it ran level by level, an Unhedged Ladder. The pre-market map, built from the previous cash session's positioning, was an Inverted Range — a call floor below price and a put lid above, both holding price in: the call 28,318 below, the Put Wall, ZG and TGF stack 28,698–28,708 above, and the largest call 28,938 above that.

  • 🟨 Open print — the first live print of the cash session set the day: price opened at 28,208, below the 28,318 floor; the 28,938 call released ≈ 70%; the puts below grew seven- to eight-fold (28,128 −17 → −128M, 27,928 −18 → −145M); the whole book turned put-side (−4.73B); SKEW 0.060, below 0.10. A Put Chase with the floor already gone — READY short, destination 27,928.
  • 🟨 9:30–9:45 — the high, 28,228, came at the open: a retest of the 98DTE put from below. Price then broke 28,128, the expected-move bottom 28,018 and the 27,928 destination in one print (≈ −460 points).
  • 🟨 10:00 — every put below roughly doubled; the largest became 27,428 (−330M). SKEW fell to 0.046 — no hedge, the ladder kept running. 27,428 was reached by about 10:20.
  • 🟨 10:45 — the largest put moved to 27,115 (≈ −800M), with σ at 0.70 and the book −17.88B, still growing ≈ 3.5B per print.
  • 🟨 11:00 — SKEW turned negative for the first time (−0.017) at the σ peak (0.87), after ≈ 1,450 points: calls priced over puts at the extreme — capitulation, not a new short. The morning low ≈ 26,74x came just before.
  • 🟨 11:15–11:30 — the book shrank for the first time (+3.74B, then +5.18B) and σ turned down (0.87 → 0.75 → 0.65): a short-covering bounce to ≈ 27,3xx, under the 27,328–27,415 lids. No calls of size appeared.
  • 🟨 12:15 — the book grew again (−4.69B); 27,115 became the largest strike of the day (≈ −1.2B), now above price as a lid. Price slid lower through the afternoon to the low, 26,619 at 15:45, on the 26,619 put, −7.0% from the prior close, and closed at 26,698.
