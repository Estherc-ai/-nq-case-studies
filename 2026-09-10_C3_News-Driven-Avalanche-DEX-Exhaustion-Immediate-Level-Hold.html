Pre-market to open transition: Pre-market DEX across all three tenors was small and near-neutral (+251 M / +88 M / −27 M), indicating no large pre-existing directional position. This distinguishes the session from an Overnight Call/Put Unwind archetype — there was no stale pre-market bet resetting at the open. The overnight decline was driven entirely by the news catalyst, and the DEX move that follows at the open is fresh positioning, not the unwind of a prior position.

SKEW compression at the open: SKEW compressed sharply from 0.231 pre-market to 0.141 at 9:30 AM and held near that level through 9:45 AM. Despite a genuine, news-driven overnight decline that broke every pre-market structural level, SKEW did not spike — it compressed immediately at the open. This is read as fear fading rather than building: the shock had already been priced into the overnight move, and the options market was not demanding growing downside protection as the RTH session began.

Sustained DEX exhaustion, 10:00 AM–1:00 PM: By 10:00 AM, 0DTE and 1DTE DEX had turned deeply negative (0DTE −2.27 B, 1DTE −1.23 B) while 7DTE remained positive (+2.69 B) — a front-end/back-end sign split. By 10:15 AM, 7DTE also turned negative, and all three tenors stayed deeply negative through the remainder of the session, with 7DTE reaching a peak of −5.61 B at 11:45 AM. SKEW remained compressed throughout this stretch (mostly 0.10–0.19), never re-expanding to price in escalating fear alongside the sustained negative DEX. Read together, this combination — deeply negative and sustained DEX without corresponding SKEW expansion — is consistent with continued put-side positioning (either sustained fresh put buying or profit-taking by traders positioned from the crash low) rather than a fear-driven panic event. The absence of SKEW expansion argues against a fresh panic spike; the persistence of the negative DEX argues against simple short-covering, since covering would be expected to push DEX back toward positive rather than deeper negative.

03 — Price Structure and the Two-Leg Decline

Following the open at 29,138, price fell quickly through the 98DTE immediate level at 29,077 to a session low of 29,057, approaching but not reaching the downside Expected Move (29,027). As SKEW compressed and DEX momentarily paused its expansion, price staged a pullback, advancing through the near-price Expected Move (29,208) toward the Total Gamma Flip / Major Wall confluence (29,302).

The pullback stopped short of 29,302 — the session high printed at 29,277, roughly 25 points below the confluence level, without a direct test. Price then reversed into a second leg lower. DEX remained deeply negative through this window (7DTE near −5B to −5.6B), and the second leg found support at the 98DTE immediate level (29,127) — a shallower low than the first leg's sweep of 29,057. The session closed at 29,142, just above the 29,127 immediate level.

The shallower second low, occurring while DEX remained near its most negative readings of the session, is consistent with the underlying negative positioning persisting without re-accelerating to a new extreme — the immediate level absorbed the pressure at a higher price than the first sweep, rather than giving way to a new session low.
