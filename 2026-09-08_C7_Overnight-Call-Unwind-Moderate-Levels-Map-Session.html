Moderate 98DTE OI concentrations — methodology:

The pre-market level stack contains two distinct categories of structural reference. Dominant labeled levels (Major/Call Wall, Put Wall, ZG, TGF, Max Pain) are identified by GBR's automated labeling system based on the largest aggregate OI concentrations across all expirations. These define where the session ultimately resolves.

Moderate 98DTE OI concentrations (29,681 / 29,631 / 29,581 / 29,481 / 29,455) are unlabeled bars visible on the 98DTE ABS GEX chart, identified manually each session by reading the strike-by-strike OI distribution within approximately 200–300 NQ points of the expected open price. Each represents a real hedging obligation at that specific strike — not as large as a dominant wall, but structurally meaningful because each level is a separate group of positioned participants defending that exact strike. Price must absorb each one sequentially rather than moving freely between dominant walls.

The practical distinction: dominant walls tell you where the session resolves. Moderate 98DTE OI concentrations tell you how it gets there — the step-ladder price climbs or descends before reaching the dominant boundary. Sep 8 confirmed this precisely: every single session turning point coincided with a pre-market marked moderate level, not the dominant Put Wall or Major/Call Wall.

Three concurrent macro drivers elevated pre-market fear: U.S.-Iran renewed military warnings, U.S.-Canada trade war escalation over the Labor Day weekend, and Friday's hot jobs report (+162K vs ~50K expected) pushing September rate hike probability to 63%. All three produced the highest pre-market 0DTE SKEW (0.340) of the post-Labor Day sequence — genuine multi-driver fear, not mechanical.

The dominant structural feature pre-market was the Put Wall (29,531) — larger than the Major/Call Wall (29,781) — creating an asymmetric setup where the downside is better defended than the upside is capped. Combined with all-positive DEX across all three tenors, the structural bias entering the session was upside-leaning despite the macro fear backdrop.
