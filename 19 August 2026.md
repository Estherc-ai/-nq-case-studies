## 03 — Session Mechanism: A Fourth Rejection at the Same Level, an Overextension Below Two Live Put Walls, and a Clean VXN-Confirmed Reversal

RTH opened at 29,696 — technically only +0.484% above the 0DTE ZG (29,553), a "mild" reading by that number alone. But the open carried real bearish context the raw ZG distance doesn't capture: price opened **below Total Gamma Flip (29,734)**, the same 98DTE aggregate regime marker that flagged a structural shift on Aug 18, and had already been **rejected three separate times pre-market** at the Large Put DEX Concentration (29,743). This wasn't a neutral, no-bias open — it was starting the session already under the longer-dated gamma structure, testing directly back into a level that had already proven it could reject price repeatedly. **This combination was a very clear signal, not an ambiguous one:** the Large Put DEX Concentration (29,743) and Total Gamma Flip (29,734) sit only 9 points apart — essentially the same zone — so the open's retest wasn't testing one level in isolation, it was testing a genuine confluence of two independent, already-bearish structural markers at once. The open effectively produced a fourth test of that combined zone: price pushed up to retest 29,743 one final time — matching the exact session high — was rejected again, and reversed hard.

From there price fell straight through ZG (29,553) and kept going. **At 9:40 AM, live put-side volume began building sharply at 29,589** — a volume event (real-time trading activity), not an OI-based level, consistent with the same live-volume-as-signal pattern documented in the two prior sessions. Separately, **two live put walls then formed at 29,489 and 29,439** — these are a different mechanism entirely: OI-based structural levels building in real time as price continued down, not volume spikes. Price broke through both of these live-forming walls, continuing to a session low of 29,377, below both.

**That break beneath the two live put walls proved to be an overextension rather than a genuine breakdown.** Price reversed quickly, reclaiming ground back up through both broken walls and eventually closing at 29,559 — just slightly above the 0DTE ZG (29,553), essentially settling back near the pivot after the full round trip below it.

**VXN confirmed the shape of the move with clean, simultaneous timing on both ends.** During the NQ low, VXN held above its own +1SD (22.57) for a sustained stretch — elevated fear tracking the price low, the standard inverse-confirmation read. Then around 10:15 AM, at the same moment NQ reversed back upward, VXN dropped below its own SD baseline — both tools flipping together at essentially the same instant, a clean two-tool confirmation of the turn rather than one lagging the other.
