1 — Market Context

The day after the Nasdaq-100's worst session since 2020. The October jobs report, released at 8:30 ET, came in far above forecast: payrolls +165,000 against about 65,000 expected, unemployment 4.1%, and August–September revised up by 84,000. At 10:00, University of Michigan consumer sentiment rose to 51.0 from 48.0, with one-year inflation expectations down to 4.3% from 4.7%. Chip stocks rebounded from Thursday's sell-off; the Nasdaq Composite closed +1.09%, the S&P 500 +0.31% and the Dow −0.46%, and VIX fell 9% to 25.64. In the NQ futures session, NQ swept to 26,372 at the Asian open, then rallied 811 points to 27,183 before the cash open — most of it before the data, little after it.

By the open both sides of the near map had already been reached overnight, and price sat on the heaviest stack of the day.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Inverted Range · Hedged — an Inverted Range is a book with a call floor below price and a put lid above, both holding price in; Hedged means SKEW stayed at 0.10 or above, protection on the book, so every move came with a pullback and none became a trend. The floor was the 0DTE Call Wall 27,154, the largest call of the session, on top of the heaviest level on the map, 27,104 — ZG, TGF, the 98DTE Put Wall and a −5.6B 98DTE put block on one strike. The lid was the put at 27,404, with 27,504 above it.

  • 🟨 9:45 — the open print set the type: calls under price, a lid above, hedged, nothing to chase — an Inverted Range · Hedged, range 27,304 ↔ 27,404 into the 10:00 data. Calls under and at price (27,154 +480M, 27,304 +430M), the put lid 27,404 (−340M) 97 above, nothing growing above the lid; SKEW 0.174 with σ down from 1.05 to 0.60 — the jobs-report premium out, protection kept. The high, 27,425, swept the lid by 21 in the first fifteen minutes.
  • 🟨 10:00 — the 27,404 lid switched to a call (−340M → ≈ +20M), but no call was growing above it: the calls that grew were below price, floors, and the nearest call above (27,374) was already reached. No destination to chase — by 10:15 price was back in the range.
  • 🟨 10:30–12:15 — a pin on the Call Wall: 27,154 (+335 to +478M) above the 27,104 put (−150 to −262M), 50 points apart. Two legs up, at 11:00 and 11:30, failed under 27,304; each time SKEW drifted toward 0.10, protection was bought again on the next print.
  • 🟨 12:45 — the Call Wall released −56% (+412 → +180M), the largest put jump of the afternoon came in, and SKEW reached its high (0.202): the low, 26,988, swept the 27,004 put by 16. With no larger put below, price returned to the Call Wall and closed at 27,094.
