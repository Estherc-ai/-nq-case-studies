The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Call Chase · Anchor (9:30, 27,236 reached overnight) → Fork (10:45).

A Call Chase is a day when the largest 0DTE call DEX sits above price and pulls it up. An Anchor is the largest strike already reached and left behind price — it no longer pulls; the open side decides. A Fork is a day when the largest call sits above price and the largest put below it at a similar size: both act as magnets, price swings between them, and the book flips print to print until one magnet is released (half of its own peak or less) while the other holds or grows. Cashed means protection (puts) was sold; protection bought means puts were bought as insurance.

  • Pre-market: a Call Chase shape. The 27,236 call (+275M, also the GEX Call Wall) above, with puts under −32M everywhere — about 8×. The 98DTE stacked behind it: a +0.27B call at 27,236, the TGF and a −0.25B put at 27,186 just under it, and the −6.45B Put Wall at 27,086. SKEW 0.132: protection held. σ 0.82.
  • Overnight: the destination was already reached. From the Asian low at 27,095 — on the 27,086 Put Wall — NQ ran to 27,609, past 27,236 and up to the Expected Move top, then gave most of it back before the open.
  • Both sides were reached before the open. The overnight range covered the top — 27,236 and the Expected Move top — and the floor — the Asian low, 27,095, on the 27,086 Put Wall. With both already used, there was no fresh destination either way: price opened inside a range the market had already covered.
  • 🟨 9:30: the anchor. The open print rebuilt 27,236 to +675M and added a rung at 27,286 (+252M), but SKEW cashed at the destination: 0.132 → 0.070 as price broke 27,236. A cash at the destination is the stop — the break failed, and price fell back under the TGF.
  • 🟨 10:00: the session high, 27,325. An overshoot of the 27,236 call by 89; the 15-minute bars then rejected 27,236 four times. The strike shrank print by print (+675M → +463M) once it had been tagged — the calls closing at the target.
  • 🟨 10:45: a Fork. The put below grew to a similar size: 26,886 −192M against 27,236 +303M (1.6×). The book flipped (+1.14B → −2.14B) and price closed under 27,086 — then the 11:00 print reversed it: the puts below released and 27,236 rebuilt to +473M.
  • 🟨 11:15: the session low, 26,922 — 36 short of the 26,886 bottom magnet. At 11:45 the puts below doubled again (26,886 −248M) and SKEW cashed (0.113 → 0.076) — and the 12:00 print reversed it again (puts below −15–36%, 27,236 +309M).
  • 🟨 12:30: cashed at the destination — again. The book flipped to +7.03B, 27,236 grew to +890M, and SKEW fell 0.117 → 0.002 as price broke 27,236 — a cash at the destination: the stop, with one last push to ≈ 27,316. By the 13:15 print the calls had released (27,236 +510M), a −150M put sat above at 27,266, and protection was bought back (0.116).
  • The Fork held all session — price stayed inside it. Neither magnet was released for two prints. Three breaks — 10:45 down, 11:45 down, 12:30 up — each reversed on the next print. Close 27,190, on the TGF (27,186), in the middle of the Fork.
