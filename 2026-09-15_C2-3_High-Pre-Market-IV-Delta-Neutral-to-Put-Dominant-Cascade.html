03Session Mechanism: Compression, a Fast Break Through the "Dominant" Wall, and Small-Stack Containment

The session's full arc reads as a single coherent story once DEX and SKEW are read together from the open through the close. Pre-market conditions looked calm — DEX flat and perfectly balanced, SKEW near the low end of its range — but 0DTE implied volatility was already elevated relative to the 30DTE tenor, a divergence that argued against taking the calm positioning data at face value. That divergence proved correct: shortly after the open, price pushed to the session high while SKEW rose and DEX turned deeply negative at the same time — a genuine, confirmed trigger, not noise. From there, price fell hard, with DEX remaining deeply negative and SKEW declining steadily throughout the drop, consistent with a mechanical cascade rather than fresh conviction. At the low, SKEW was still falling and DEX was still deeply negative — the selling pressure had not yet released even though the immediate move had paused. The extended range that followed was a genuine fight at that level: DEX remained deeply negative for hours without resolving, and the small 98DTE stack held regardless, absorbing the unreleased pressure structurally rather than DEX/SKEW confirming any exhaustion. Only into the afternoon did conditions genuinely ease, with SKEW continuing its decline toward 0.057 and DEX finally shrinking from billions to negative millions — the real release, arriving hours after the price action itself had already stabilized.

The session opened at 29,420, sitting below the 0DTE Gamma Flip, and traded in a struggle around that zone through the first thirty minutes — visible on the chart as repeated chop rather than a clean directional resolution in either direction. Between roughly 9:45 and 10:00 AM, VXN printed a sharp move from 21.47 to 22.43 on the blend engine — a whip through the −2σ/−1σ zone and back up toward +1σ within the same minute — coinciding with price reaching the session high (29,453) in that same window. DEX-weighted registered the more precise exact −2σ touch during this whip, though the shape of the move (a sharp sweep followed by immediate reversal) was the meaningful signal across engines.

At 10:00 AM, 0DTE DEX flipped decisively from its flat pre-market baseline to −609.19M, with composition shifting to a put-dominant 36% call / 64% put and SKEW spiking from 0.089 to 0.223 — a genuine, same-minute conviction signal arriving at the exact price level (29,420) that had been contested for the prior half hour. This was the trigger. From 10:15 through 11:15 AM, 0DTE DEX escalated further (−759M → −1.26B → −1.34B → −1.14B → −1.44B) with composition sliding increasingly put-dominant (32/68 → 20/80 → 18/82 → 21/79 → 13/87), while SKEW fell monotonically the entire time, from its 0.223 peak down to 0.079 — never once ticking back up. This is a textbook Mechanical Cascade: rising (more negative) DEX paired with steadily falling SKEW, consistent with gamma-driven dealer hedging as price fell through successive levels rather than fresh option buying. A newly-formed immediate wall at 29,419 and the pre-market 98DTE/0DTE Put Wall at 29,319 — nominally the single largest structural level on the entire board — were both broken through in quick succession during this cascade, with no meaningful struggle at either.

Price fell continuously through the cascade and bottomed at 29,216 by 10:45 AM — an event that coincided with VXN reaching exactly +3σ on the blend engine, the same-minute convergence that had marked the high roughly an hour earlier, with the blend engine uniquely hitting its band precisely while other engines (including DEX-weighted) overshot past their own +3σ levels rather than touching cleanly. From 10:45 AM, price ranged for approximately four hours between 29,216 and 29,306 — the small, manually-identified 98DTE open-interest stack — while 0DTE DEX remained negative but decayed gradually in magnitude through the afternoon, from the billions down to the negative millions, and VXN slowly bled from +3σ toward +1σ over the same stretch without ever fully releasing back to the anchor. Near 4:00 PM, a final wick dipped slightly below the range to print the true session low (29,209) — a second, same-minute-confirmed touch that represented the cleanest point in the session to have taken profit on the earlier short thesis. The session closed at 29,276, back inside the 29,216–29,306 containment zone.

The central finding of the session: nominal wall size did not determine which level held. The 98DTE Put Wall was the largest bar on the pre-market chart by a clear margin, yet it broke almost immediately once genuine DEX/SKEW conviction confirmed the move — while the small, stacked 98DTE cluster below it, individually unremarkable in size, contained price for the remainder of the session. Per the framework's own Stack Walls principle, multiple adjacent smaller concentrations can prove harder to break through than a single larger one, since each represents a separate hedging obligation that must be absorbed in sequence rather than overcome in one move — and DEX/SKEW behavior, not raw open interest, was what actually determined the outcome at both the large wall and the small stack.
