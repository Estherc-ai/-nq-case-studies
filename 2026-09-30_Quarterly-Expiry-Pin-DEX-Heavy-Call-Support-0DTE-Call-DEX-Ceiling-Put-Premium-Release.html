🟨 9:30 — Open above 30,694: support

30,694 was the strongest level near the open: the largest 98DTE Net GEX call and the largest positive DEX on the same strike. Price moved above it before 9:30. The open printed at 30,699; the first bar's wick swept just below 30,694 and held. With pre-market SKEW at 0.039 (no put premium) and a call-weighted 0DTE book (69 / 31), the call wall acted as support rather than resistance. By 10:00, DEX(OI) rose to +3.2B (83 / 17) and SKEW to 0.100 — both rising, the squeeze direction.

🟨 10:15 — First rejection at 30,869: put premium spike and release

The 76-point rise from 10:00 to 10:15 repriced delta on the existing book (open interest is fixed intraday). The 0DTE calls at 30,869 moved toward at the money and became the largest DEX above price, while 30,769 and 30,694 moved in the money below. Price wicked to 30,893 and rejected at 30,869. SKEW jumped from 0.100 to 0.348 while DEX fell from +3.2B to +1.77B — put premium bought at the wall. On the next print (10:30), SKEW released to 0.129 while call DEX rebuilt to +3.3B (85 / 15). A fast put-premium release with no conviction behind the call side priced 30,869 as the range ceiling rather than a breakout level. 10:15 is a single-print spike; Sep 30 was also the JHEQX quarterly collar roll date, but the prints cannot attribute the spike to it.

🟨 1:45 — Second put wave inside the pin

From 11:30, DEX cycled: it fell to +2.85B on the first put wave (11:45, SKEW 0.130) and rebuilt to +13.25B by 12:15. The 0DTE call DEX at 30,869 stayed the largest strike at every print (≈ +1.0B to +2.5B). At 1:45, DEX fell to +2.11B with put DEX concentrated at 30,769 (≈ −0.82B), 30,849 (≈ −0.73B) and 30,809 (≈ −0.45B), and SKEW fell to 0.027 — put flow with no put premium, mechanical. 30,769 held. By 2:15 the put block had mostly cleared (DEX +15.48B, SKEW −0.001).

🟨 3:30 — Release into settlement

From 10:15 to 3:30, 30,869 rejected price 6+ times on wicks only (high 30,906). On quarterly expiry, gamma at the heaviest strike rises into settlement, so the ceiling held with each touch instead of weakening. At 3:30 the pin released: price flushed to 30,639 by ≈ 3:45, through 30,694 and the SD baseline, then closed at 30,726, above 30,694. The release is consistent with out-of-the-money delta at 30,769–30,849 decaying into PM settlement (charm), removing the hedge support under price; no print was captured after 2:15.
