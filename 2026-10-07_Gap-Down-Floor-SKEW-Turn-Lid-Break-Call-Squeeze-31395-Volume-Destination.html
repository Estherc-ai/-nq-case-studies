🟨 10:00 — Floor confirmed

🟨 11:00 — Reversal ready

🟨 11:15 — Call squeeze confirmed

At 12:45 DEX recovered to +11.69B and calls rebuilt at 31,350 (+1.18B) and 31,400 (+455M): the pause held, and price finished at the volume destination — high 31,416, close 31,406.
