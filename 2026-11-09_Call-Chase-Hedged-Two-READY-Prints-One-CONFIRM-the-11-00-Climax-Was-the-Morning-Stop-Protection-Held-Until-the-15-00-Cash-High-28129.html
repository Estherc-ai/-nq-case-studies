1 — Market Context

Tech led a broad rebound from the AI-driven sell-off earlier in the week, and crude fell about 3% after comments played down the risk of renewed strikes on Iran. The Nasdaq-100 closed +3.40%, the Nasdaq Composite +2.74%, the S&P 500 +2.35% and the Dow +2.15%, with about three in four Nasdaq-100 names higher. There was no major data on the calendar — Fed speakers and low-impact releases only — with CPI due the next morning. In the NQ futures session, price swept to about 26,77x at 3:00 ET and rallied about 580 points into the cash open, stopping exactly at the 27,352 largest put.

By the open the put lids below the map were behind price, and the only large call on the map — 27,452 — sat 112 points above.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Call Chase · Hedged — a Call Chase is a day when price is pulled toward the largest call above it, rung by rung; Hedged means SKEW stayed at 0.10 or above from the open to 14:45 — protection kept on the book — so the chase came with pauses and pullbacks, and no top became a reversal. The open-print destination was the 0DTE Call Wall 27,452. Each destination, once reached, became a floor, and a new call grew above it: 27,679 → 27,802 → 27,892 → 28,002.

  • 🟨 9:30 — the open print set the type and the side: READY LONG → 27,452, the largest call, 81 above price and growing (+178 → +590M). A READY, not a CONFIRM: it was the first live print, so nothing could yet grow two prints in a row, and price still sat under TGF. 27,452 was reached within minutes.
  • 🟨 9:45 — a new call, 27,679 (≈ +430M), grew above the old destination — the move was not over. Still a READY, not a CONFIRM: price was 18 points under the new destination, and SKEW was falling toward 0.10, so 27,679 could have been the top.
  • 🟨 10:00 — CONFIRM LONG, hedged ladder: 27,679 was passed and turned into a floor (+550M), the next rungs grew on a second print (27,802 +110 → +235M, 27,892 +85 → +140M), DEX jumped +2.87B and SKEW was bought back (0.122 → 0.157) instead of cashing. Price touched the 27,679 floor at 10:06 and held.
  • 🟨 11:00 — the climax print: the largest one-print DEX jump of the session (+4.62B), price ≈ 27,984 through the 27,892 Call Wall, 18 under the 28,002 destination (+600M). The morning stop. At 11:15 the calls above released (28,002 −38%, 28,102 −65%) and price fell 84.
  • 🟨 11:15 → 12:00 — protection held at the top (SKEW 0.134 → 0.184): a hedged pullback to the call floors, not a reversal. At 12:00 a new +4.12B jump and a growing 28,002 brought price back.
  • 🟨 15:00 — protection was cashed for the first time in the session; the last leg broke 28,102 to the high, 28,129, at 15:15, and price closed at 28,005.
