1 — Market Context

The day after the US midterm elections. AP projections showed Democrats winning majorities in both the House and the Senate. In the NQ futures session overnight, NQ first sold off to 27,609, below the expected-move bottom, then rebounded to 28,664 by about 7:00 ET, into the old flip and call cluster at 28,642–28,692. US crude fell more than 8% to $88.12. AMD, reporting after Tuesday's close, rose early and finished +1.01%; Nvidia closed −3.17%. The Dow closed +3.03% (its best day since April 2025), the S&P 500 +1.58% and the Nasdaq Composite +1.44%. NQ had closed Tuesday's cash session at 28,312.

Both pre-market extremes had already been reached overnight — the open print had to show which side was still the destination.

The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Call Chase · Hedged — a Call Chase is a book whose largest strike is a call above price, with nothing of similar size pulling from below, so price is pulled up to it; Hedged means protection (SKEW ≥ 0.10) stayed on the book, so the moves came with pullbacks rather than one way. The pre-market map, built from the previous cash session's positioning, was a Fork — a book with its largest call above price and its largest put below, two magnets of similar size: here the call 28,662–28,667 above and the put pair 27,832–27,842 below. A pre-market map carries the last session's book, so a large overnight move can change the type — and the overnight session had already swept both sides.

  • 🟨 9:32 — the open print, the first live print of the cash session, decided it: the call 28,667 grew to ≈ +405M, a second call at 28,132 grew to ≈ +305M below as a floor, and the put pair released ≈ 45%. Price opened at 28,428, just above ZG 28,422, swept it to 28,406 and held — the open print showed a Call Chase to 28,667, a READY with the destination 159 above.
  • 🟨 9:45 — price passed the destination; protection cashed below 0.10, but Monday's high and the rungs above were still untagged, so the cash fuelled the run.
  • 🟨 10:15 — the climax print (+3.78B in one print), high 28,972, through Monday's high 28,892 and the 28,942 lid. The biggest DEX jump of the day, with no bigger call above to pull price on, marked the top — and with protection held at 0.159 (no cash), it marked the start of a hedged pullback, not the end of the up move.
