03 — Session Mechanism: Nearest Wall First at the Open, VXN ±2.5σ Sweeps Bracket the Session

Rule confirmed this session: at the open, price targets the nearest available wall first — not the largest. RTH opened at 29,113 and immediately swept lower to 29,041 — targeting the nearest structural level (large put concentration at 29,055), not the deeper levels further below. At the exact same minute, VXN swept through +2.5σ (21.62) and reversed. The cross back through +2σ (21.54) confirmed the exhaustion was genuine.

VXN exhaustion hierarchy confirmed: a ±2σ touch alone is ambiguous — price may stall there or push toward ±2.5σ next. The ±2.5σ sweep is the meaningful signal — reaching there requires sustained vol expansion, and the reversal back through ±2σ carries more mechanical force. Use ±2σ cross-back as the confirmation, not the ±2.5σ touch itself.

VXN's first −2.5σ sweep (20.49) coincided with price reaching the 29,255 OI confluence zone — a rejection as expected given the level's confirmed size. Price pulled back then pushed higher again. At 11:50 AM, VXN swept −2.5σ (20.49) a second time, and in the same 2–3 minute window NQ printed the session high at 29,317, coinciding with the live-adjusted Total Gamma Flip + Major Wall (NQ 29,321, 4-point miss). The second sweep distinguished the true destination from the false one at 29,255: when the right structural level is the genuine destination, VXN signals it within 2–3 minutes.

Cumulative delta divergence was visible through the recovery: volume exceeded overnight levels but price recovered only approximately half the session's total range without making a higher high — absorption, not genuine buying conviction.

In the afternoon, price made a second leg lower to the session low of 29,002. VXN exceeded +3σ (21.71) while skew remained compressed at 0.18–0.19 — symmetric vol expansion, not panic. The 29,055 zone held the afternoon test as it held the morning open sweep, before price recovered to close at 29,138.
