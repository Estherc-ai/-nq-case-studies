🟨 10:15 — Put-floor release

The first prints flipped the call-heavy book to puts (DEX −729M, SKEW 0.042 → 0.111) while σ fell — protection, not fear. The floor formed at the bottom of the expected move: puts concentrated at 31,195–31,205 (≈ −98M each) as the puts further below unwound. At 10:00 puts were rebuilt across the ladder (31,175 −12M → −101M, 31,145 −13M → −88M) and price swept to 31,157 inside that target before reclaiming 31,195. At 10:15 DEX flipped from −1.74B to +643M, the puts below unwound again (31,145 turned to a call) and 31,245 grew from +93M to +375M as price broke through it — the floor was confirmed and the move ran without a pullback.

🟨 10:30 / 10:45 — ZG call-DEX peak and call-DEX decay above the flip

DEX jumped to +7.78B and SKEW fell to 0.077: calls chased. The heaviest growth was at price — 31,345 from +185M to +695M — making 31,345 / the ZG the destination; price topped at 31,370. Calls were also building further above (31,445–31,470), but those were contingent "if" strikes. The 10:45 print showed no real buyers above the flips: 31,345 collapsed to +117M, the calls at 31,445 and 31,470 halved and a new put appeared at the ZG (−75M). By 11:00 the 31,345 call had flipped to a put and the ZG was the lid.

🟨 12:15 — Put re-accumulation at the floor

Price slid back to the 31,195 floor by 12:15. The legend looked like a release setup (SKEW 0.014, σ at the session low 0.16), but the strike bars at nearly the same price showed puts building at the floor: 31,195 from −510M to −860M, 31,205 from −245M to −670M. The 12:15 candle wicked into 31,295 and was rejected; the 12:30 print bounced to +959M as price retested 31,245, but it did not hold. At 12:45 DEX fell to −13.18B, σ rose for the first time in the session (0.16 → 0.19) and the 31,195 floor broke. After two flat 1-minute candles at the floor, price fell without a pullback to 31,045, bounced off 30,995 and made the low at 30,794 near 13:30.
