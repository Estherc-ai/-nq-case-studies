The stop was trailed down as each subsequent wall was confirmed broken on the way down, tying risk management directly to structural confirmation rather than a fixed distance.

04b — Two New Execution Rules Established This Session

Wall-break timing: only the first ~30 minutes of a session appears to carry enough force to break a genuinely defended wall — both the London/NY 28,625.62 sequence (broke at the open) and the 9:34 AM put wall (held for the rest of the session, formed just past the 30-minute mark) are consistent with this. Outside that window, the higher-probability read is rejection, not breakthrough — favoring taking profit into a level rather than expecting continuation through it.

Trailing stop to the last confirmed broken wall: rather than a fixed distance, moving the stop to just beyond the most recently broken structural level ties risk management directly to price proving a level has given way, avoiding both premature stop-outs on normal noise and giving back the move on a reversal.
