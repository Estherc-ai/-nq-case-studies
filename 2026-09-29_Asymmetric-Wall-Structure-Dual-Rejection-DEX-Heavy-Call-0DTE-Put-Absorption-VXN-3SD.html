🟨 9:30 — Open at 30,708: rejection

30,708 was the strongest level near the open: the largest DEX strike above price (≈ +1.72B) and a GEX call wall, and it had already rejected price twice on Sep 28. A rejection was the expected first reaction. Price opened on it, swept it by 17 (high 30,725) and rejected. By 9:45, opening flow was put-heavy (−536.3M, BOTH) on a near-balanced OI book, and SKEW collapsed from 0.164 to 0.023 — a mechanical flush with no put conviction. It stopped at the put wall (≈ 30,578) after sweeping the 30,560 Asian-open baseline, and reversed.

🟨 10:45 — Second touch of 30,708: call-side release

At 10:30, DEX and SKEW rose together into the wall (+2.08B to +2.56B; SKEW +0.039 to 0.093) — the squeeze direction. At the 10:45 touch, both fell (DEX −1.15B to +1.41B; SKEW −0.023 to 0.070) — a call-side release, and price rejected. The largest call strike, 30,683, peaked at +740M at 10:30, shrank to +450M at 10:45, and flipped to a −936M put by 11:30. From 11:00 to 11:30, DEX went from −0.29B to −11.45B while SKEW fell from 0.095 to 0.060 — a mechanical cascade.

🟨 12:30 — Exhaustion into the live put wall

From 11:30, DEX cycled between −8.2B and −11.6B while SKEW fell at every print, to 0.042 at 12:30 — large put flow with no put premium. The 12:30 print showed both falling (exhaustion). The 0DTE OI book stayed near balanced (+123.8M, 53 / 47), so the negative DEX in the live prints came from same-day volume. A new put mass formed at 30,513–30,523 (−301M / −317M) — the live 0DTE put wall at 30,519. The low printed at 30,503 at 12:45: a wick below 30,519 with closes held above. 30,483 was not reached.

🟨 2:00 — End of the absorption

From 12:45 to 2:00, the 0DTE OI book stayed between −331M and +260M, split around 45 / 55 — a flat, near-balanced book while price held 30,519. SKEW alternated between 0.042 and 0.102, with a defense print at 1:00 (DEX −155M, SKEW +0.048 to 0.090). At 2:00 both fell (−237M, SKEW 0.046) — exhaustion. At 2:15, DEX turned positive (+87M) with SKEW at 0.098, and price moved up to close at 30,650.
