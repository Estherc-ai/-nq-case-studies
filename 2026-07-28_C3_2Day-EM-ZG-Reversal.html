04 — Session Mechanism: Wall Sweep-and-Reject, Then a Clean Round Trip to the 2-Day Expected Move

RTH opened at 27,951, just below ZG (28,050) — a mild Condition 3 open. Price pushed up immediately and swept through both the new live put wall (27,972, formed right at the open) and the primary Put Wall (28,000) — the two largest walls in play at that moment — before rejecting at both. From there, price reversed hard, breaking down through the secondary Put Wall (27,820) and continuing straight toward the IV 2-Day Expected Move zone (~27,600), bottoming at a session low of 27,604 — a 4-point miss on a level that was read visually off a chart with no printed number, the second consecutive session this tool has called the bottom almost exactly (Jul 27: predicted ~27,600 vs. actual 27,605; today: same predicted zone vs. actual 27,604).

From the low, price reversed and ran all the way back up to ZG (28,050), then settled into a range around that level for the remainder of the session. The whole-session high (28,080) sits only 30 points above ZG — confirming the reversal found real resistance right at the gamma-flip level rather than continuing through it, consistent with ZG's role as the day's structural pivot once price returned to it.

VXN's behavior tracked this shape closely: it spiked toward and briefly through +3SD (30.16) during New York AM, in the same window as the wall-sweep-and-flush move, then declined steadily through the session, settling into a range around −1SD (28.59) during New York PM as price stabilized near ZG — both instruments converging on calm, range-bound behavior in the same afternoon window.

Skew told a genuinely different story on this session than prior write-ups — rather than a single compress-or-widen trend, it whipsawed in step with the session's own multi-phase shape:
