The session opened already pinned within 10 points of a strike carrying both the largest put and call concentration on the board — the signature of a monthly options expiration rather than an ordinary session. Overnight, the London session had already advanced to 29,967 before pulling back below that same strike ahead of the RTH open — an early signal that the pin carried real gravitational pull well before the New York session began. What followed was not primarily a story of price discovery, but of a large, heavily call-weighted position expiring, the dealer hedge built against it being unwound, and the market absorbing that unwind before resolving into a genuine directional move. DEX and SKEW, read against the mechanics of options expiration, tell this story far more precisely than the price chart does on its own.

The open and the failed rally. Price opened at 29,814, already above the monthly Put/Call Wall pin (29,804), and attempted to extend toward the large 98DTE Call concentration (29,854) within the first few minutes. That attempt failed at 29,846 — eight points short — and reversed. The 0DTE DEX figure at this moment (+5.02B) reflects the AM-settled monthly position, not fresh directional flow: an option approaching a Special Opening Quotation settles gradually as the underlying index's component stocks each print their own official opens, and the smooth decay visible in the table (+5.02B → +1.78B → +1.01B → +1.3M between 9:30 and 10:30 AM) is that settlement process completing, not a change in market conviction.

Why the settlement itself produced real market pressure. A heavily call-dominant position of this size means dealers had spent the preceding sessions holding a large long hedge — underlying bought specifically to offset their short-call obligation, per standard delta-hedging mechanics. Once the option cash-settles, that obligation disappears, but the hedge built against it does not vanish with it: the dealer is left holding a residual hedge with no remaining position to justify it, and sells the leftover accordingly. This is mechanical inventory-clearing, not a market view — the residual hedge of a position whose original purpose has already been resolved.

The consolidation as absorption, not indecision. From roughly 10:00 AM to 12:15 PM, price ranged inside a defined band while 0DTE DEX cycled repeatedly — swelling toward the billions on each small up-leg (+1.78B, +1.01B, +1B), collapsing toward flat on each pullback (+1.3M, +365M) — without a clean directional resolution in either direction. Read alongside VXN's three failed attempts to ease down over the same stretch, both tools were showing the same underlying condition from different angles: the hedge-unwind supply from the expired monthly position being worked through the market gradually, rather than dumped at once, with neither DEX nor VXN able to resolve cleanly until that absorption was complete.

Consolidation Detail — MNQZ26, 15m

The session low. At 12:15 PM, price wicked to the session low (29,660) before closing the same candle back above 29,704, a secondary structural level. SKEW spiked sharply at this exact moment (0.128 at 11:00 AM → 0.212 at 12:15 PM) while 0DTE DEX remained comparatively modest (+258M) — a genuine, if brief, capitulation coinciding with the wick rather than a sustained breakdown building underneath it. The absence of structural support did not mean the absence of a reason to expect a hold; the options data supplied that reason where the chart could not.

The reversal, and the mechanical signature behind it. By 12:45 PM, 0DTE DEX had surged back to +1.01B while SKEW collapsed to 0.155 — DEX rising sharply as SKEW falls is the same Call-side mechanical signature documented elsewhere this cycle, distinguishing a dealer-hedging-driven advance from one built on fresh directional conviction. VXN's fourth failed-to-reject breakdown at 13:06, discussed in Section 02, preceded price's eventual break above the consolidation range and reclaim of the Asian Open baseline. Price ultimately swept well past the Expected Move upside boundary (29,934) to a session high of 29,994, closing at 29,960.

Session summary. The failed open-range rally, the choppy consolidation, the unsupported low, and the reversal each appear as disconnected price events when read in isolation. Attributing the session's structure to a single identifiable mechanism — a heavily call-weighted position approaching scheduled settlement, the resulting dealer hedge unwinding into the market, that unwind being absorbed gradually through the late morning, a brief capitulation completing the absorption, and a new directional cycle beginning once the mechanical supply was exhausted — resolves these into a single coherent sequence. This is also consistent with Friday positioning reflecting funds establishing exposure ahead of the following week, though that final element remains an inference from timing and context rather than a directly confirmed mechanism.
