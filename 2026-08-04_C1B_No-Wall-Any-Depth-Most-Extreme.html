04 — Session Mechanism: Every Structural Ceiling Cleared, Spread Confirms the Break

RTH opened at 29,233 — +1.509% above ZG, the most extreme Stratospheric open logged this cycle, exceeding even Jul 30 and Jul 31. Price dipped briefly to test the 1-Day Expected Move zone (~29,218) before turning decisively higher.

What followed was a genuinely rare structural picture: price ran through every wall at every depth of the term structure — the 0DTE Call Wall, the 98DTE Major Wall, the 190DTE and 365DTE aggregate walls (29,118.43), and continued into the 2-Day Expected Move / Asian +1SD confluence zone (29,318.43/29,332.06). At that zone, spread was visibly wide pre-market — the dealer-uncertainty signature from a genuinely contested level. Once the session opened, that spread tightened back to normal, and price continued straight through — the exact "resolved uncertainty, direction confirmed" pattern from the microstructure framework: wide spread at a level followed by a tight spread and continuation means the fight was won decisively, not stalled.

Sequential live call walls then formed and cleared, one after another, as price advanced — a repeating stepping-stone structure rather than a single breakout point, extending the "price chases volume" pattern documented on Jul 30 into a continuous, multi-level version of the same mechanism. Even above the 3-Day Expected Move (29,418.43), live volume and wall structure kept forming and continued leading price higher — the volume didn't fade or thin out once price cleared every pre-calculated statistical reference; it kept building, reinforcing that this was sustained, real demand rather than a move running out of participation. Price ultimately reached a session high of 29,956, clearing not just every OI wall available but also the 3-Day Expected Move by over 500 points and Asian +2SD (29,733.12) by 223 points — a move that outran every pre-calculated statistical reference this framework has, at any horizon.

The session held those gains almost entirely — the only real give-back was a small pullback right near the close, which is where the session's technical low (29,208) actually printed, not at any point earlier in the day. In other words: for the overwhelming majority of the session, price never traded meaningfully below its opening level; the "low" is a late, minor retracement rather than an early flush.

Skew's behavior adds the key distinction between this squeeze and Jul 30's. At the very start of the session, skew read negative — an unusual, unsettled signature. But rather than trending upward alongside the squeeze the way Jul 30's skew did (0.265 to 0.324), skew here stabilized to a near-flat 0.030 by 9:45 AM and held there. That's a materially different squeeze signature: Jul 30 was a squeeze with building fear underneath it (rising skew); Aug 4 was a squeeze with a settled, orderly options market underneath it (stable skew). Same core mechanism — VXN rising with price, dealers hedging short-call exposure — but a calmer, more digested backdrop this time, consistent with the session's overwhelmingly one-directional shape and the minimal, late-session-only pullback.
