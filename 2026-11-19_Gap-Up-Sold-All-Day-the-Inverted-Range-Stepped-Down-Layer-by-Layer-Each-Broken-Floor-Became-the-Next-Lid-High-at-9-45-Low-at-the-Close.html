The day in brief. 🏷️ Day type: Inverted Range (9:30) → box stepped down (10:15) → Put Chase · Unhedged (12:00).

An Inverted Range is a day when the largest 0DTE put DEX sits above price and the largest call DEX below it. The edges hold price inside the box, and a door opens only when an edge is gone in full. The outer box is the largest put above ↔ the largest call below. The inner box is the nearest put above ↔ the nearest put below. A Put Chase is a day when the largest put sits below price and pulls it down. Released means a strike has fallen to half of its own peak or less: it no longer holds or pulls. Cashed means protection (puts) was sold; protection bought means puts were bought as insurance. Unhedged means the move runs with no protection behind it. TGF is the total gamma flip.
