DEX and SKEW were both sustained and elevated throughout the entire squeeze window: DEX never faded, and critically, SKEW never trended down toward exhaustion — the same-direction confirmation required for a Genuine Squeeze classification, distinguishing today from Sep 16's Mechanical Cascade, where SKEW's steady decline was the tell that conviction was absent.

A short entered at that level was covered profitably as price fell back toward the session low zone (29,606), consistent with the VXN whip correctly flagging exhaustion of that push.

During the pullback to the session low — observed live shortly after the 9:32 AM VXN whip, ahead of the first tabulated 15-minute snapshot — DEX and SKEW both eased together, a same-direction decline matching the framework's Call-side Unwind/Release signature. Following this, the squeeze documented in the table above resumed and carried price back into the 29,706–29,756 zone, where it ranged for the remainder of the session without a further reversal, closing at 29,721 — inside the call concentration stack, just above the 29,706 level.
