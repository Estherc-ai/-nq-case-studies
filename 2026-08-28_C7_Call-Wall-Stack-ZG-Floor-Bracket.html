03 — Session Mechanism: Put Wall Swept Immediately, Call Wall Stack Caps Every Push, VXN Confirms the ZG Floor

RTH opened at 29,627 — +0.424% above the 0DTE ZG (29,502), and immediately swept down through the small, live-confirmed-weakest 0DTE Put Wall (29,622) on the open, consistent with that level's already-flagged lack of real defense. From there price approached the 0DTE ZG (29,502) closely but did not touch it on the first pass, reversing back upward.

Price then pushed up through the Major/Call Wall confluence (29,562) and the new mid-size call wall (29,702), running to a session high of 29,812 — landing just short of the largest live call wall (29,822), the top of the resistance stack. From that high, price reversed back down toward the ZG zone a second time.

At 10:13 AM, VXN swept through +2SD (20.83) and reversed back below it; in that same minute, NQ traded at 29,506 — essentially at the 0DTE ZG (29,502) — before reversing back up toward the session high again. This same-minute VXN/price convergence confirmed the ZG zone as genuine support rather than a level in the process of breaking. Price ultimately settled back down into the close, finishing at 29,507 — once again right at the ZG level.

Open interest ran high from the very start of the session, and the overall shape was a clean bracket day: the three-level call wall stack (29,562 / 29,702 / 29,822) capped every upside attempt, while the 0DTE ZG (29,502) held every downside attempt, producing a session that oscillated almost entirely between 29,502 and 29,822 — a textbook stack-vs-floor structure with VXN's +2SD sweep-reversal providing independent confirmation of the floor at the moment it mattered most.
